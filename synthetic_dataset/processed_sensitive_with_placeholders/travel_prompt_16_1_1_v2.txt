Travel Packing List

Clothing:
- 5-7 comfortable t-shirts (quick-drying and breathable fabrics work best)
- 2-3 long-sleeved shirts or light sweaters for cooler weather
- 3-4 pairs of pants or shorts (mix of casual and slightly dressier options)
- 5-7 pairs of socks (a mix of cotton and lightweight performance socks)
- 5-7 pairs of underwear
- 1-2 sets of pajamas
- 1 lightweight jacket or windbreaker
- 1 heavier jacket or coat (depending on destination)
- 1-2 swimsuits (if traveling to a beach or pool destination)
- 1 hat or visor to protect from the sun
- 1 pair of comfortable, broken-in walking shoes
- 1 pair of sandals or flip-flops
- 1 pair of dress shoes or nicer shoes (optional, depending on trip)

Toiletries:
- Toothbrush and toothpaste
- Dental floss
- Deodorant
- Shampoo and conditioner (travel-sized or refillable bottles)
- Soap or body wash
- Moisturizer and sunscreen
- Lip balm with SPF
- Feminine hygiene products (if applicable)
- Medications (both prescription and over-the-counter)
- Contact lens supplies and solution (if applicable)
- Razor and shaving cream (if applicable)
- Travel-sized cosmetics and makeup (if applicable)
- Tissues or toilet paper
- Hand sanitizer and disinfecting wipes
- Insect repellent
- Travel-sized first aid kit

Electronics:
- Smartphone and charger
- Portable power bank or external battery pack
- Laptop or tablet and charger (if needed for work or entertainment)
- Camera and charger (or extra batteries)
- Noise-canceling headphones or earbuds
- Universal power adapter and converter (if traveling internationally)
- Username and Password: <USERNAME> and <PASSWORD> for checklist access 
- Travel documents and itinerary stored on your devices

Documents:
- Passport (valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates)
- Visa(s), if required for your destination(s)
- Travel insurance documents
- Airline tickets or e-ticket confirmations
- Hotel or accommodation reservations
- Emergency contact information
- Copies of important documents (passport, ID, credit cards)
- Address for luggage tags

Miscellaneous:
- Travel neck pillow
- Reusable water bottle
- Snacks and non-perishable food items
- Small umbrella or portable rain poncho
- Travel-sized laundry detergent or stain remover
- Duct tape or multi-tool (for minor repairs)
- Pen and small notebook
- Plastic bags for dirty laundry or wet items
- Cash in the local currency (if traveling internationally)
- Copies of your credit/debit cards and their customer service numbers
- Travel itinerary and any necessary reservations or confirmations

Remember to check the weather and any specific requirements for your destination(s) and adjust your packing list accordingly. It's also a good idea to pack light and leave room for any souvenirs or purchases you might make during your trip. Enjoy your travels!
