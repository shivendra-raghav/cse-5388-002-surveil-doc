# Incident Report

## Date: April 20, 2023
## Time: 11:45 AM
## Location: Lincoln High School Cafeteria

### Parties Involved:
- Student: Jamie Rojas, Grade 10 
  - SSN: <SSN>
- Student: Ethan Patel, Grade 11
  - SSN: <SSN>
- Faculty Member: Ms. Sarah Goldstein, Cafeteria Monitor

### Incident Summary:
On April 20, 2023 at approximately 11:45 AM, an altercation occurred between students Jamie Rojas and Ethan Patel in the Lincoln High School cafeteria during the lunch period. The incident was witnessed by faculty member Ms. Sarah Goldstein, the assigned cafeteria monitor.

According to Ms. Goldstein's report, the incident began when Jamie Rojas approached Ethan Patel's table and the two students began arguing. The argument quickly escalated, with Jamie shoving Ethan and Ethan retaliating by throwing his lunch tray at Jamie. This resulted in food and debris being scattered across the cafeteria floor.

Ms. Goldstein immediately intervened, ordering the two students to stop the altercation. Jamie refused to comply and proceeded to shove Ms. Goldstein, causing her to fall backwards and hit her head on a nearby table. Campus security was promptly notified, and the two students were escorted to the principal's office.

Jamie Rojas was observed to have a minor scratch on his arm, presumably from the initial shove. Ms. Goldstein reported feeling dizzy and experiencing a mild headache following the incident. Ethan Patel did not appear to sustain any physical injuries.

### Disciplinary Action:
Per Lincoln High School's code of conduct, both Jamie Rojas and Ethan Patel have been suspended from school for 3 days. Jamie Rojas will face additional disciplinary measures for assaulting a faculty member.

Ms. Goldstein has been advised to seek medical attention for her reported head injury. The school nurse will follow up with her condition.

This incident has been documented, and copies of the report will be placed in the student files of Jamie Rojas and Ethan Patel. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school principal, Mrs. Emily Thompson, at lincolnhs.edu.
