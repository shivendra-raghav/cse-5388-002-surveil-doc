E-Commerce Company User Onboarding Documentation

Introduction
Welcome to our e-commerce platform! This comprehensive user onboarding documentation will guide you through setting up your account, managing your information, accessing key resources, and contacting our support team when needed. Our user-friendly interface and robust features are designed to streamline your e-commerce experience, allowing you to effortlessly buy and sell products online.

Account Management Steps
1. Account Creation
   a. Visit our website at www.shopnowonline.com
   b. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner
   c. Enter your personal details, such as name, email address, and create a secure <PASSWORD>
   d. Agree to our terms and conditions
   e. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox

2. Account Login
   a. Return to our website at www.shopnowonline.com
   b. Click on the "Login" button in the top-right corner
   c. Enter your registered email address as the <USERNAME>
   d. Enter your secure <PASSWORD>
   e. Click "Log In" to access your account dashboard

3. Account Dashboard Overview
   a. Your account dashboard provides an overview of your recent activity, orders, and messages
   b. From the dashboard, you can navigate to different sections like "My Profile," "My Orders," and "My Listings"
   c. Ensure all your personal and payment information is up-to-date in the "My Profile" section

4. Managing Your Listings
   a. To create a new product listing, click on the "Create Listing" button in the top menu
   b. Fill out the product details, including title, description, pricing, and upload high-quality images
   c. Set shipping options, handling times, and any additional information buyers should know
   d. Review and publish your listing once you're satisfied with the details

5. Tracking Orders
   a. The "My Orders" section displays a comprehensive list of all your orders, both as a buyer and seller
   b. Click on an order to view its details, including shipping status, tracking information, and communication history
   c. You can leave feedback on completed orders to help build your reputation on our platform

Access List
1. Knowledge Base
   Our extensive knowledge base is a valuable resource containing tutorials, FAQs, and troubleshooting guides. Access it by clicking on the "Help" button in the top menu and selecting "Knowledge Base."

2. Community Forums
   Engage with fellow buyers and sellers, ask questions, and share insights on our vibrant community forums. Access the forums by clicking on the "Help" button in the top menu and selecting "Community Forums."

3. Live Chat Support
   For immediate assistance, our dedicated support team is available via live chat. Access the live chat by clicking on the "Help" button in the top menu and selecting "Live Chat."
