Trip Overview
This 10-day trip to Italy will take you on a journey through the country's rich history, stunning landscapes, and vibrant culture. Starting in Rome, you will explore the ancient wonders of the Eternal City before making your way to the picturesque Amalfi Coast. From there, you will venture to the charming town of Cinque Terre, where you can hike between the colorful villages and enjoy the breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. The trip will conclude in the fashion capital of Milan, where you can indulge in world-class cuisine, go shopping, and visit iconic landmarks.

Activities
Rome:
In Rome, you will visit the iconic landmarks of the Colosseum, the Roman Forum, and the Trevi Fountain. You will also explore the Vatican City, including St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel. Additionally, you will take a guided walking tour of the city's charming neighborhoods, such as Trastevere and the Jewish Ghetto.

Amalfi Coast:
On the Amalfi Coast, you will spend time exploring the picturesque towns of Positano, Amalfi, and Ravello. In Positano, you can hike the Sentiero degli Dei (Path of the Gods) for stunning views of the coastline. In Amalfi, you can visit the impressive Duomo di Amalfi and explore the charming streets. In Ravello, you will visit the Villa Rufolo and the Villa Cimbrone, both famous for their beautiful gardens and panoramic vistas.

Cinque Terre:
In Cinque Terre, you will hike the scenic trails that connect the five villages: Monterosso, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola, and Riomaggiore. You can also take a boat ride along the coast to admire the colorful houses and stunning cliffs from the water. Additionally, you will have the opportunity to visit local vineyards and sample the region's renowned wines.

Milan:
In Milan, you will visit the iconic Duomo, one of the largest Gothic cathedrals in the world. You will also explore the city's fashion district, known as the Quadrilatero della Moda, and go shopping at high-end stores and boutiques. Additionally, you will visit the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, a stunning 19th-century shopping arcade, and the Sforza Castle, a historic fortress that now houses several museums.

Dining
Throughout your trip, you will have the opportunity to indulge in the rich and diverse cuisine of Italy. In Rome, you will enjoy authentic Roman dishes, such as carbonara, amatriciana, and cacio e pepe, as well as classic pizzas. On the Amalfi Coast, you will savor fresh seafood, homemade pasta, and the region's renowned lemons in the form of limoncello liqueur and lemon-based dishes. In Cinque Terre, you will sample the local pesto, focaccia, and seafood specialties. Finally, in Milan, you will explore the city's vibrant culinary scene, including traditional Milanese dishes like risotto alla Milanese and ossobuco.

Notes
Remember to bring comfortable walking shoes, as you will be doing a significant amount of walking and hiking during this trip. Additionally, it's a good idea to familiarize yourself with the local customs and etiquette in each destination to ensure a respectful and enjoyable experience.
