Claims Filing

In the event that you need to file a claim for covered services, our goal is to make the process as straightforward and efficient as possible. Here are the steps to follow:

1. Obtain an itemized statement or invoice from your healthcare provider, which should include the following information:
   - Your name and member ID (or <SSN>)
   - Date(s) of service
   - Description of services rendered
   - Diagnosis codes
   - Procedure codes
   - Charges for each service

2. Complete a claim form, which can be obtained from our member portal or by contacting customer service.

3. Attach the itemized statement or invoice to the claim form.

4. Submit the completed claim form and supporting documentation to the address provided on the form or through our secure online claims submission portal at claims.healthylifeinsurance.com.

Please note that claims must be submitted within 90 days from the date of service. Late submissions may result in denial of the claim.

If you have any questions or need assistance with the claims filing process, our customer service representatives are available to guide you through the steps.
