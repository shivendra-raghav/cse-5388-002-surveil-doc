Benefit Overview

Our healthcare plan offers a wide range of benefits designed to meet your healthcare needs. Here's a summary of what's covered:

- Preventive Care: Annual check-ups, routine screenings, and immunizations are covered at 100% when you visit an in-network provider.
- Physician Services: Office visits, specialist consultations, and surgical procedures are covered with a $25 copay.
- Hospital Services: Inpatient and outpatient hospital services are covered with a 20% coinsurance after deductible.
- Prescription Drugs: Our plan includes a comprehensive prescription drug benefit with varying copays of $10/$30/$50 based on the drug tier.
- Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services: Our plan covers mental health and substance abuse treatment services with a $35 copay.

For a detailed overview of your plan benefits, please refer to the Summary of Benefits and Coverage document included in your welcome packet.

Exclusions and Limitations

While our plan provides comprehensive coverage, there are certain exclusions and limitations you should be aware of:

- Pre-existing conditions may have waiting periods or limited coverage during the first 12 months of your plan.
- Cosmetic procedures and services are generally not covered unless deemed medically necessary.
- Experimental treatments or therapies not approved by the relevant governing bodies may not be covered.
- Out-of-network services may be subject to higher out-of-pocket costs or limited coverage.

Please review the full list of exclusions and limitations in your plan documents to ensure you understand the scope of your coverage.
