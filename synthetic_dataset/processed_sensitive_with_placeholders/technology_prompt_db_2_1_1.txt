## Backup Details

Regular backups of the MongoDB database are essential to ensure data integrity and prevent data loss in case of system failures or disasters. Our organization follows a comprehensive backup strategy to safeguard the data stored in the MongoDB instances.

### Backup Schedule

- Full backups are performed weekly on Sundays at 2:00 AM.
- Incremental backups are performed daily at 1:00 AM.

### Backup Storage

All backups are stored on a secure, off-site storage system hosted by [Cloud Storage Provider] with redundancy and encryption measures in place. The backup storage location is regularly monitored and maintained to ensure data availability and accessibility in case of a disaster recovery scenario.

### Backup Retention Policy

- Full backups are retained for 6 months.
- Incremental backups are retained for 30 days.

## Cluster Information

Our MongoDB deployment is configured as a sharded cluster to ensure high availability, scalability, and performance. The cluster consists of the following components:

### Config Servers

- `config1.mongodb.com:27019`
- `config2.mongodb.com:27019`
- `config3.mongodb.com:27019`

The config servers store the metadata for the cluster and provide configuration data to the other components.

### Shards

- `shard1/replica1.mongodb.com:27017`
- `shard1/replica2.mongodb.com:27017`
- `shard1/replica3.mongodb.com:27017`
- `shard2/replica1.mongodb.com:27018`
- `shard2/replica2.mongodb.com:27018`
- `shard2/replica3.mongodb.com:27018`

The shards are responsible for storing the actual data. Each shard is a replica set consisting of three nodes for redundancy and high availability.

### Query Routers

- `router1.mongodb.com:27017`
- `router2.mongodb.com:27017`
- `router3.mongodb.com:27017`

The query routers act as the entry point for client applications and direct queries to the appropriate shards based on the configured sharding strategy.
