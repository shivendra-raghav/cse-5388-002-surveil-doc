Dear David,

I hope this email finds you well. As we discussed, I wanted to provide you with an update on Exelon's completed acquisition of a 49.9% stake in Sithe Energies.

The key details are as follows:
- Exelon Corporation and Sithe Energies Inc. have announced the successful completion of Exelon's acquisition of 49.9% of Sithe's stock. 
- The remaining 50.1% of Sithe will be owned by Vivendi (34%), Marubeni Corp. (15%), and Sithe Management (1%).
- Vivendi Universal retains a blocking minority interest of 34% in the company, and PECO Energy (now part of Exelon) becomes Sithe's main shareholder.
- PECO has also been given a three-year commitment starting from December 2002 to buy Vivendi Universal's remaining interest.

This acquisition is an important strategic move for Exelon as it continues to expand its generation portfolio. Exelon estimates the initial investment in Sithe will contribute approximately $0.01 per share to its consolidated earnings in 2001 and 2002. And if Exelon were to purchase the remaining 50.1% of Sithe (as soon as two years from now), it anticipates the transaction would contribute approximately $0.15 to its annual consolidated earnings.
