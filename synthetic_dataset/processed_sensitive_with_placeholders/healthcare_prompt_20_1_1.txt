Inpatient Hospital Care

Outpatient Care

• Physical, occupational, and speech therapy

Emergency Services

Prescription Drug Coverage

Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services

The specific coverage details, limitations, and exclusions for each of these services can be found in your plan documents.

Out-of-Pocket Costs
While ABC Healthcare covers a significant portion of your healthcare expenses, you may be responsible for certain out-of-pocket costs, depending on the services you receive and the healthcare providers you visit.

Deductible
Your plan has an annual deductible of $750 for individual coverage and $1,500 for family coverage. This means you'll need to pay the full cost of your covered services until you reach your deductible amount. Some preventive care services are exempt from the deductible and covered in full.

Copayments
After meeting your deductible, you'll be responsible for copayments for certain services. For example:

• Primary care physician visits: $20 copay
• Specialist visits: $35 copay
• Urgent care visits: $45 copay
• Emergency room visits: $150 copay (waived if admitted)

Coinsurance
For some services, you'll need to pay a percentage of the cost, known as coinsurance. For instance, you may be responsible for 15% coinsurance for inpatient hospital stays after meeting your deductible.

Out-of-Pocket Maximum
To protect you from excessive healthcare costs, your plan has an annual out-of-pocket maximum of $4,000 for individual coverage and $8,000 for family coverage. Once you reach this limit, ABC Healthcare will cover 100% of your eligible expenses for the remainder of the plan year.

Please note that premiums, non-covered services, and certain other costs do not apply toward your out-of-pocket maximum.

Claims Filing
If you receive healthcare services from an in-network provider, they will typically file the claim with ABC Healthcare on your behalf. However, if you visit an out-of-network provider, you may need to submit the claim yourself.

To file a claim, please follow these steps:

1. Obtain an itemized bill from the healthcare provider, including the following information:
   • Your name and <SSN>
   • Provider's name and contact information
   • Date(s) of service
   • Description of services rendered
   • Diagnosis codes
   • Procedure codes
   • Total charges

2. Complete the claim form, which can be found on our website or obtained by contacting our Customer Service department.

3. Attach the itemized bill and any other supporting documentation to the claim form.

4. Submit the claim form and supporting documents to ABC Healthcare within 90 days of the date of service. You can mail the documents to:

ABC Healthcare Claims Department
P.O. Box 789012
Cityville, State 12345

Or, you can submit your claim electronically through our secure online portal.

5. Once your claim is processed, you will receive an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) statement outlining the covered amount, your out-of-pocket costs, and any additional information or actions required.

If you have questions or need assistance with filing a claim, please contact our Customer Service department at 1-800-555-ABCARE (1-800-555-2222).
