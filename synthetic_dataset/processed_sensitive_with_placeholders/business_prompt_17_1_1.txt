Request for Proposal (RFP)
Procurement of Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Software

Introduction:

ABC Corp is seeking proposals from qualified vendors for the implementation of a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system to streamline and integrate our business processes across various departments, including finance, accounting, human resources, supply chain management, and inventory management.

Our company is a leading Automotive organization with 5,000 employees and 25 locations worldwide. We are committed to leveraging cutting-edge technologies to enhance operational efficiency, improve decision-making processes, and provide exceptional service to our customers.

Scope of Work:

The selected vendor will be responsible for the following tasks:

1. Conduct a detailed analysis of our current business processes and requirements, and recommend an ERP solution that best aligns with our needs.
2. Design, develop, and implement a customized ERP system tailored to our specific business operations.
3. Integrate the ERP system with our existing systems, such as Customer Relationship Management (CRM), Business Intelligence (BI), and other third-party applications.
4. Migrate historical data from legacy systems to the new ERP system, ensuring data integrity and accuracy.
5. Provide comprehensive training and knowledge transfer to our employees to ensure seamless adoption and effective utilization of the ERP system.
6. Offer ongoing support, maintenance, and upgrades for the ERP system to ensure its long-term sustainability and alignment with our evolving business needs.

Timeline:

The project should be completed within a timeframe of 18 months, with the following milestones:

1. Requirements gathering and analysis: 6 weeks
2. System design and development: 9 months
3. Data migration and integration: 8 weeks
4. User Acceptance Testing (UAT): 6 weeks
5. Training and deployment: 8 weeks
6. Post-implementation support: 12 months

Evaluation Criteria:

Proposals will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

1. Technical expertise and experience in implementing ERP systems for organizations of similar size and complexity.
2. Proposed solution's ability to meet our specific business requirements and scalability for future growth.
3. Quality of project management methodology, including risk mitigation strategies and change management processes.
4. Vendor's financial stability, industry reputation, and customer references.
5. Total cost of ownership (TCO), including licensing fees, implementation costs, and ongoing support and maintenance costs.
6. Vendor's commitment to data security, compliance with industry regulations, and protection of sensitive information.

Submission Instructions:

Interested vendors should submit their proposals to John Smith at procurement@abccorp.com by June 30, 2023. Proposals should include the following:

1. Executive summary
2. Company overview and qualifications
3. Proposed ERP solution and implementation approach
4. Project timeline and milestones
5. Team structure and resource allocation
6. Training and knowledge transfer plan
7. Support and maintenance strategy
8. Cost proposal with detailed pricing breakdown
9. Customer references and case studies
