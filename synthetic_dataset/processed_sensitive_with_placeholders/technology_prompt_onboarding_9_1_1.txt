Employee Details

OnboardMe simplifies the employee onboarding process by collecting and managing essential information. Here's how to add new employees to the system:

1. Log in to the OnboardMe application using your administrator <USERNAME> and <PASSWORD>.
2. Navigate to the "Employees" section and click on the "Add New Employee" button.
3. Fill in the required fields, including personal information, job details, and emergency contact information.
4. Review the entered information for accuracy and click "Submit."

Once an employee is added to the system, OnboardMe generates a unique <USERNAME> and <PASSWORD> for them. These credentials will be used by the employee to access their personal onboarding portal and complete any remaining tasks or documentation.
