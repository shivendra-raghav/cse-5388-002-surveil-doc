Brand Guidelines
- Pinnacle Solutions' brand identity is represented by a sleek and modern logo featuring a stylized letter "P" in shades of blue and gray. The color palette consists of various shades of blue (Hex: #003366, #0066CC, #00A3E0) and gray (Hex: #4D4D4D, #808080, #CCCCCC), which convey professionalism, reliability, and innovation.
- The primary font for the brand is Montserrat, a modern sans-serif typeface that exudes simplicity and legibility. Headings and titles should use bold or semi-bold weights, while body text should be set in regular or light weights.
- The brand tone of voice should be confident, knowledgeable, and approachable. Marketing communications should be informative yet engaging, highlighting the company's expertise and commitment to delivering cutting-edge solutions.
- The company's name, logo, and other branding elements must be used consistently across all marketing materials, including website, print collateral, presentations, and digital assets.
