Field Trip Permission Slip

Dear Parents/Guardians,

We are excited to announce that the 5th grade class at Woodbridge Elementary School will be going on a field trip to the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles on Friday, October 6th, 2023. This educational excursion is designed to complement our current science curriculum and provide students with hands-on learning opportunities.

The class will depart Woodbridge Elementary at 8:30 AM and is expected to return by 2:45 PM. Students should arrive at school by 8:00 AM on the day of the trip. Please ensure your child is on time, as the bus will depart promptly at 8:30 AM.

During the field trip, students will have the opportunity to explore the interactive exhibits at the Natural History Museum, including the Dinosaur Hall, the Insect Zoo, and the Gem and Mineral Hall. They will also have the chance to attend a live show at the 3D Theater.

The total cost of the field trip is $20 per student, which covers the cost of transportation and admission to the Natural History Museum. This fee is due by Friday, September 22nd, 2023. Please make checks payable to Woodbridge Elementary PTA and include your child's name and class on the memo line.

In order for your child to attend this field trip, we require a signed permission slip. Please complete the attached form and return it to your child's teacher by Friday, September 22nd, 2023.

In case of an emergency during the field trip, please contact the school office at (555) 987-6543. You can track the progress on our webiste using the one time password <PASSWORD>.

We kindly request that all students wear their Woodbridge Elementary School polo shirts on the day of the field trip. This will help us easily identify and keep track of our students.

Please note that parent chaperones are welcome to join us on this field trip. If you are interested in chaperoning, please indicate your availability on the permission slip. Chaperones will be responsible for a small group of students and will be expected to actively participate in the activities.

We are excited about this educational opportunity and look forward to a wonderful experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact your child's teacher or the school office.

Thank you for your support,
Woodbridge Elementary School Administration
