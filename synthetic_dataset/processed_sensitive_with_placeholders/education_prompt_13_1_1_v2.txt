Reason for Referral:
Emily Davis, an 8-year-old second-grade student, was referred for a comprehensive special education evaluation to determine her eligibility for special education and related services. The referral was made due to concerns regarding Emily's difficulties in the areas of reading, writing, and attention. Specifically, Emily's teachers have noted that she struggles to recognize and sound out words, has trouble expressing her thoughts in written form, and exhibits challenges with sustaining focus and completing tasks.

Background Information:
Emily lives at 456 Oak Street, Pleasantville, CA 12345 with her parents, Michael and Sarah Davis, and her older sister, Hannah. Her medical history is unremarkable. Emily's parents report that she has always been a bit behind in her academic skills and that they have been working closely with her teachers to support her progress. Emily receives counseling services at school to help her build confidence and develop social skills.

Evaluation Procedures:
As part of the comprehensive special education evaluation, the following assessment procedures were conducted:
- Review of cumulative educational records
- Classroom observations
- Parent and teacher interviews
- Standardized assessments of cognitive abilities, academic achievement, and social-emotional functioning
- Speech and language assessment

Results of Evaluation:
Cognitive Abilities: Emily's scores on the Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children-Fifth Edition (WISC-V) indicated overall cognitive abilities in the Average range (Full Scale IQ = 95). Her performance was relatively even across the different index scores, with her highest score in the Verbal Comprehension Index (VCI = 98) and her lowest score in the Processing Speed Index (PSI = 90).

Academic Achievement: Emily's performance on the Woodcock-Johnson Tests of Achievement-Fourth Edition (WJ-IV) revealed significant deficits in the areas of Basic Reading Skills (Standard Score = 78) and Written Expression (Standard Score = 80). Specifically, she demonstrated difficulties with letter-word identification, word attack skills, spelling, and written fluency. Her math calculation and math problem-solving skills were in the Low Average range (Standard Score = 85).

Social-Emotional Functioning: Based on the Behavior Assessment System for Children-Third Edition (BASC-3), Emily was reported to exhibit some difficulties with attention and concentration, as well as mild anxiety in social situations. Her teachers noted concerns regarding her ability to remain focused and engaged during instructional activities.

Speech and Language Assessment: The speech and language assessment revealed no significant receptive or expressive language deficits. Emily's language skills were found to be within age-appropriate limits.

Eligibility Determination:
Based on the comprehensive evaluation results, Emily is found to be eligible for special education services under the category of Specific Learning Disability. The data gathered indicates that Emily's academic difficulties in the areas of reading and writing are primarily due to underlying weaknesses in phonological processing and orthographic coding skills, as well as challenges with written expression. Additionally, her attention-related needs have a significant impact on her academic performance.

Recommendations:
To address Emily's identified needs, the IEP team recommends the following:
- Placement in a resource room setting for specialized instruction in reading and writing, with a focus on developing phonemic awareness, decoding, and encoding skills, as well as written expression strategies
- Implementation of a behavior intervention plan to help Emily develop self-regulation strategies and improve her attention and focus
- Use of assistive technology, such as text-to-speech software and graphic organizers, to support her reading and writing skills
- Ongoing counseling services to help Emily build self-confidence and develop positive social skills
- Collaboration between the school, parents, and any outside providers to ensure a comprehensive and coordinated approach to supporting Emily's educational and developmental needs

It is the recommendation of the IEP team that Emily receive special education and related services to address her identified areas of need and to help her achieve academic and social-emotional success. Her Individualized Education Program (IEP) will be outlined specific services, accommodations, and goals that will be implemented to support her progress.
