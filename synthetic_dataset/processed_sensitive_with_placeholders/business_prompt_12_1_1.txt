ACME Corp Marketing Plan 2023

Introduction
This marketing plan outlines the strategy for promoting Pranksters Paradise's line of novelty products in the coming year. Our offering includes classic prank items like hand buzzers, whoopee cushions, and disappearing ink as well as new innovations like x-ray novelty glasses and wildly overacting pill cameras.

The marketing objectives for 2023 are:
• Increase brand awareness by 25%
• Grow online sales by 40%
• Expand into 5 new major retail chains
• Launch 2 new product lines

ACME has an annual marketing budget of $750,000 to execute campaigns, promotions, advertising, events, and other initiatives. With creative thinking and strategic execution, we can achieve our lofty goals while staying within budget.

Market Analysis
The novelty item industry is highly competitive but also extremely lucrative with total annual revenue over $3 billion. ACME has traditionally performed well, capturing 12% market share and ranking as the #2 novelty brand behind industry titan Prank King Corp.

There are relatively low barriers to entry for small startups to launch competing products. However, ACME has strong brand equity, customer loyalty, trade relationships, and manufacturing expertise built over 60+ years. We are well-positioned to maintain our leadership position through continued investment in R&D, marketing, sales, and quality control.

A new trend shaping the market is the rise of digital/virtual pranks and interactive novelty experiences accessed through apps and websites. This "prank-as-a-service" model still represents a small portion of the market (6%) but is rapidly growing as millennials and Gen Z drive demand.

Some of our key competitors include:

• Prank King Corp ($1B revenue, 37% market share)
• TrickCo Novelties ($340M revenue, 11% market share)
• Jake's Prank Shop ($180M revenue, 5.3% market share)
• Tons of small Etsy/Amazon sellers

Target Audience
Our primary customer personas are:

Pranksters (35%) - Ages 12-24, both male and female with a slight skew towards males. This segment loves staging harmless pranks on friends and family, whether in-person or through sharing recorded stunts online. Pranksters have an edgy sense of humor and enjoy anything over-the-top, outrageous, or controversial that gets a big reaction.

Gag Gift Givers (30%) - Ages 25-55, skews towards higher incomes. This group loves giving funny and surprising novelty gifts for holidays, birthdays, office parties, white elephant exchanges, etc. Each year they look for the hottest new items to delight recipients and maybe prank them at the same time.

Magicians & Entertainers (15%) - Ages 20-45, skews towards males, mix of hobbyists and professionals. They seek out prop novelties to incorporate into close-up magic shows and live performances.

While covering a wide age range, the common thread across all target segments is a love for laughter and never taking life too seriously. These consumers are open to surprise and delight, have a lighthearted attitude, and want to add a bit of playfulness to their lives or events.

Marketing Mix
To reach our ambitious goals, we'll execute a multi-channel marketing mix leveraging both digital and traditional tactics:

Product: Innovation is key to staying ahead of the competition. We'll launch two new product lines: "Augmented Pranks" (AR novelties) to capitalize on virtual prank trends, and "Brain Games" (logic puzzles, mind tricks) to expand our market appeal beyond just gag gifts.

Promotions: We will execute attention-grabbing promotions like:
• Pranks Gone Wrong video contest with $100K grand prize
• Major celebrity endorsements and unboxing campaigns on YouTube/TikTok
• BOGO 50% off sitewide sales on Black Friday/Cyber Monday

A loyalty program called "Prank Points" will incentivize repeat purchases and help gather customer insights for remarketing.

Place: We will focus on expanding our brick-and-mortar presence at retail with a major push into 5+ new national chains like Target, Kroger, Meijer, and Walgreens. Merchandise displays will feature branded signage and video demo loops.

Enhanced store locators on our website and mobile apps will help drive foot traffic to retail partners as well as our 17 company-owned prank shops. We will expand distribution relationships with e-commerce giants like Amazon, which now accounts for 42% of our online sales.

Price: Our premium pricing requires constant monitoring, analysis, and adjustments. We'll use value-based pricing methods with different-priced good/better/best versions of each product line. New bundled multi-packs and variety sets at different price points will help maximize impulse purchases.

Tight supply chain controls and vertical integration of manufacturing functions helps reduce costs. This allows us to stay highly profitable while keeping items priced well below our closest competitors like Prank King.

Promotion: Traditional marketing channels like print, TV, radio and OOH still drive massive awareness among our demos. We'll run holiday and seasonal promo blitzes in conjunction with popular cable channels like Comedy Central, Nickelodeon, and Spike TV.

Digital marketing and e-commerce engines will be critical to driving online sales growth. Paid media efforts across YouTube, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat and other platforms will keep us visible among young demos. SEO and omnichannel campaigns will boost site traffic and conversions.

An aggressive content marketing program on our blog, email newsletters, and social channels will add value by educating consumers on the art of pranking. Influencer campaigns with top YouTube pranksters like ThePrankShow, MrJunior, Jillian Tuberid, and NELK boys will add authenticity.

Physical experiences like "Prank Zones" at theme parks and malls get people hands-on with products while generating massive social media buzz. Talent recruiting for new "PrankMasters" will give fans a chance to star in our commercials or digital content.

Budget
Here is our annual $750,000 marketing budget broken down by channel:

• Digital Ads: $200,000
• Paid Media: $150,000
• Content & Influencers: $75,000
• Physical Retail Marketing: $60,000
• PR & Events: $50,000
• Print/TV/Radio: $125,000
• Email/CRM: $25,000
• Strategic Initiatives: $65,000

This budget reflects a roughly 60/40 split between digital and traditional marketing. It allows us to go big on content, events, and amplification while maintaining solid saturation across media channels where our target consumers spend time.

A separate product development budget will cover costs for innovating new prank products lines like Augmented Pranks and Brain Games. Existing operational budgets cover staff, tools, agencies, and analytics systems needed to execute campaigns and measure ROI.

Conclusion
As the leader in novelty gift and prank products, ACME is well-positioned to deliver on our goals for 2023. By staying hyper-focused on innovation and anticipating trends, we can extend our dominance over rivals like Prank King. With a bold multi-channel marketing mix tailored to our target pranksters, gift givers, and entertainers, we will captivate audiences through surprise, delight, and bursts of pure silliness.

This comprehensive plan establishes a roadmap for building brand love and affinity through every touchpoint. From viral stunts to mind-bending brain games, we'll create products and content that get people laughing while adding unexpected joy to their daily lives. If flawlessly executed with creative genius, we'll solidify ACME as the iconic prank brand for this generation and beyond.
