Covered Services

Our healthcare plan is designed to cater to your diverse needs, offering a wide range of covered services. These include but are not limited to:

- Preventive Care: Regular check-ups, immunizations, and screenings to promote early detection and prevention of potential health issues.
- Primary Care: Access to a network of highly qualified primary care physicians for routine medical needs and referrals.
- Specialist Care: Coverage for consultations with specialists, such as cardiologists, oncologists, and orthopedists, upon referral from your primary care physician.
- Hospitalization: Inpatient care for surgeries, treatments, and extended stays when necessary.
- Emergency Care: Coverage for emergency room visits and urgent care services in case of accidents or sudden illnesses.
- Mental Health Services: Access to licensed therapists, counselors, and psychiatrists for mental health support.
- Prescription Drug Coverage: A comprehensive prescription drug plan to help manage the costs of necessary medications.

Prescription Benefits

Our prescription drug coverage is designed to ensure you have access to the medications you need at an affordable cost. Here's what you need to know:

- Formulary: We maintain a comprehensive formulary, or list of covered medications, that is regularly updated to include the latest and most effective treatments.
- Tiers: Medications are categorized into different tiers, with varying co-pay amounts based on their generic or brand-name status, as well as their cost-effectiveness.
- Mail-Order Option: For your convenience, you can opt for a 90-day mail-order supply of maintenance medications, saving you time and money.
- Prior Authorization: Certain medications may require prior authorization from our team to ensure they are medically necessary and appropriate for your condition.

Claims Process

We strive to make the claims process as seamless as possible, ensuring you receive the reimbursement you're entitled to in a timely manner. Here's what you need to know:

- In-Network Providers: When you visit healthcare providers within our network, they will typically handle the claims process directly with us, minimizing the paperwork on your end.
- Out-of-Network Providers: If you visit a provider outside our network, you may need to submit a claim form along with the relevant documentation, such as receipts and medical records.
- Claims Submission: You can submit claims electronically through our online portal or by mail, ensuring a secure and efficient process.
- Explanation of Benefits (EOB): After your claim is processed, you will receive an EOB detailing the services covered, the amount paid by the plan, and any remaining balance you may owe.
