The Notification Service Microservice is a crucial component responsible for sending notifications to users via various channels such as email, SMS, and push notifications. This microservice follows the Circuit Breaker pattern, which is a well-established design pattern used to prevent cascading failures and improve the overall system's resilience.

The Circuit Breaker pattern is particularly useful when a microservice depends on one or more remote services or third-party APIs. It helps to prevent the application from repeatedly trying to execute an operation that is likely to fail, thus allowing it to continue operating without disruption.
