Acme Corp Disaster Recovery Plan

## Introduction

This Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) establishes procedures to recover Globex Corporation's critical IT infrastructure and operations in the event of a disaster or service disruption. The main objectives of this plan are:

- Minimize interruptions to normal operations
- Limit the extent of disruption and damage
- Minimize economic impact of the interruption
- Establish alternative means of operation in advance
- Train personnel with emergency procedures
- Provide for smooth and rapid restoration of service

The plan identifies failures, assesses risks, and documents the recovery procedures to follow in different scenarios. It also defines roles and responsibilities for an effective prevention, response, and recovery process.

## Risk Assessment

To create an effective disaster recovery strategy, we must understand the specific risks that Globex Corporation faces. We have identified and assessed the following potential disruptive threats:

**Natural Disasters:** Hurricanes, earthquakes, wildfires, and other environmental incidents could damage facilities and system infrastructure.

**Technical Failures:** Server crashes, software corruption, cyber-attacks, and network outages can cripple business operations.  

**Human Errors:** Mistakes made by employees or contractors can expose vulnerabilities and lead to data loss or service disruptions.

**Service Provider Outages:** Prolonged issues with third-party services like cloud providers, internet backbone, or utilities could impact operations.

Each risk is analyzed based on its probability of occurring, the potential damage, and the impact on business operations and revenue. Mitigation strategies and recovery procedures are developed accordingly.

## Recovery Strategies 

This section outlines the strategies and procedures to follow for recovering IT systems, data, and operational capabilities after a disaster event.

### Data Backup and Recovery

Critical data assets are backed up nightly to a redundant offsite location using encryption and the <API_KEY> to authorize the backup process. In the event of data loss, backups can be restored to bare-metal servers or cloud instances.

### System and Network Recovery 

Core system infrastructure, including servers, network equipment, and telephony systems, are designed for high availability and automatic failover to a secondary data center using the <API_KEY> to trigger the failover process. Virtual machine snapshots and system images are regularly updated to expedite recovery.

Cloud services and third-party SaaS platforms must be reconfigured manually if impacted. Cloud provider APIs with <API_KEY> authentication enable automated provisioning and recovery.

### Application Recovery

Business-critical applications are clustered across multiple sites for redundancy. In a recovery scenario, traffic is rerouted using global load balancing, and new application instances are deployed from version-controlled source code and data replicas.

### Alternate Workplace Areas

If the primary workplace is unavailable, staff can be temporarily relocated to designated alternate recovery sites equipped with workstations, phones, and network connectivity. A mission-critical subset of employees may need to work remotely from home offices.

## Communication Plan

Effective communication is critical during disaster situations. This plan identifies key contacts, roles, and communication methods to ensure all impacted parties are properly informed and coordinated throughout an incident.

### Emergency Contacts

- **Incident Response Team:** Response coordinators, management sponsors, and subject matter experts who analyze the situation and manage the recovery process.
- **Employees:** Internal communications to update staff on the situation, provide instructions, and disseminate announcements using email, phone trees, emergency notification systems, and company intranet.
- **Customers and Partners:** Updates and impact statements shared through various channels like email, websites, social media, and third-party communication services.
- **Suppliers and Vendors:** Communication regarding service levels, orders, shipments, and payments through account managers and procurement teams.
- **Authorities:** Emergency services, law enforcement, regulatory agencies, and any other relevant government entities as needed.

Communication is a crucial aspect of incident management. The Incident Response Team will coordinate all internal and external communications throughout the recovery efforts.

## Testing and Maintenance

To validate recovery capabilities and ensure the plan remains up-to-date and effective, we will:

- Schedule annual disaster recovery testing of critical systems, including full restore from backups
- Perform regular tests and audits of backup integrity using <API_KEY> to access backup data
- Update plan with any changes to personnel, technical infrastructure, or emergency contacts
- Review plan after any actual recovery event to identify areas of improvement
- Conduct awareness training and emergency procedure drills for personnel

An outdated or untested plan compromises Globex Corporation's ability to respond to real incidents. Continuous review and validation are essential to maintain a viable disaster recovery capability.

## Conclusion 

This Disaster Recovery Plan provides a comprehensive guide to restore Globex Corporation's critical IT infrastructure and maintain business operations in the event of an emergency or disruption. By documenting procedures, roles, and technical requirements, the organization is better prepared to respond swiftly and minimize downtime.

However, the plan is only effective through continued maintenance and testing. As systems evolve and new risks emerge, this living document must adapt accordingly. It should serve as a vital component of Globex Corporation's overall business continuity strategy and corporate resilience.
