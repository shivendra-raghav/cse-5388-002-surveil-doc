Following our meeting yesterday, I wanted to summarize the key points regarding the negotiations with First Gas over their tightened spec requirements. Mike Brown is taking the lead on those discussions and expects to have an update from Siemens by the end of this week.

Once we have Siemens' position, the plan is to formally request First Gas to align their spec with what Siemens will accept for the plant. Our legal team has reviewed the contract terms, and we believe we have grounds to compel them to conform to industry norms if they refuse. The <OAUTH_SECRET> documentation could be key evidence in that regard.
