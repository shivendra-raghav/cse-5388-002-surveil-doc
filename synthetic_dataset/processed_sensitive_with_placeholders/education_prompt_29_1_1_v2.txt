Parent Volunteer Handbook

Welcome to Apple Grove Elementary's volunteer program! We are grateful for the time and energy our parent volunteers contribute to support our students and staff. This handbook outlines the policies and procedures for our volunteer program to ensure a positive and productive experience for all.

Volunteer Eligibility and Screening
All volunteers must complete a volunteer application and undergo a background check prior to service. This includes providing your social security number: <SSN>. Background checks help ensure the safety and security of our school community. Volunteers must also attend a brief orientation session to review school policies and procedures.

Volunteer Opportunities
There are many ways parents can get involved at our school! Volunteer opportunities include:
- Classroom Assistance: Help teachers with tasks like making copies, organizing supplies, or working one-on-one with students.
- School Events: Assist with setup, staffing, and cleanup for events like book fairs, science fairs, fun runs, and more.
- Grounds & Facilities: Help with gardening, landscaping, minor repairs, and campus beautification projects.
- Parent-Teacher Organization: Join the PTO and contribute your time and talents to school improvement initiatives.
- Chaperone Field Trips: Accompany classes on educational outings to provide extra supervision and support.

Volunteer Expectations
We ask that all volunteers:
- Sign in and out at the main office upon arrival and departure.
- Wear a visitor badge at all times while on campus.
- Respect the confidentiality of students and staff. Do not share private information.
- Follow all school rules and policies, including those related to student discipline, safety, and acceptable use of technology.
- Be reliable and punctual. Inform the school if you are unable to fulfill a scheduled volunteer commitment.
- Dress appropriately for an educational setting.
- Refrain from using cell phones or being distracted by personal matters while volunteering.

Benefits of Volunteering
Volunteering is a wonderful way to get involved in your child's education and support our school community. Some of the benefits include:
- Building connections with teachers, staff, and other families.
- Gaining a deeper understanding of school operations and initiatives.
- Modeling civic engagement and a commitment to education for your children.
- Contributing your unique skills, interests, and perspectives to enrich our programs.
- Earning credit toward school volunteer hour requirements.

Thank you for your willingness to volunteer! We appreciate your partnership and look forward to working with you. Please let us know if you have any questions or need additional information.
