Apple Inc. Intellectual Property Policy

I. Introduction

This Intellectual Property Policy ("Policy") establishes the guidelines and procedures for the management, protection, and commercialization of intellectual property created by employees, contractors, and other personnel associated with Apple Inc. ("Company"). The Policy aims to safeguard the Company's intellectual property rights while promoting innovation and fostering an environment that encourages creativity and technological advancements.

II. Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the following definitions apply:

1. "Intellectual Property" refers to creations of the mind, including but not limited to inventions, patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, know-how, and other forms of proprietary information.

2. "Invention" means any new and useful process, machine, manufacture, or composition of matter, or any new and useful improvement thereof, that is patentable under the laws of the United States or other applicable jurisdictions.

3. "Work" refers to any copyrightable material, including but not limited to literary works, software, databases, audiovisual works, and other original works of authorship.

4. "Trade Secret" means information, including a formula, pattern, compilation, program, device, method, technique, or process, that (a) derives independent economic value, actual or potential, from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable by proper means by, other persons who can obtain economic value from its disclosure or use; and (b) is the subject of efforts that are reasonable under the circumstances to maintain its secrecy.

III. Ownership

A. Inventions

1. The Company shall own all rights, title, and interest in and to any Inventions developed by employees, contractors, or other personnel within the scope of their employment or engagement with the Company, or that result from the use of Company resources, facilities, equipment, or proprietary information.

2. Employees, contractors, and other personnel are required to promptly disclose all Inventions to the Company and cooperate in the preparation, filing, prosecution, and maintenance of patent applications and patents.

B. Works

1. The Company shall own all rights, title, and interest in and to any Works created by employees within the scope of their employment with the Company, or by contractors or other personnel engaged to create such Works for the Company.

2. Employees, contractors, and other personnel are required to promptly disclose and assign all rights in and to such Works to the Company.

C. Trade Secrets and Confidential Information

The Company shall own all rights, title, and interest in and to any Trade Secrets and Confidential Information developed or acquired by employees, contractors, or other personnel in the course of their employment or engagement with the Company.

IV. Confidentiality

A. Obligation of Confidentiality

Employees, contractors, and other personnel are obligated to maintain the confidentiality of the Company's Intellectual Property and Confidential Information, both during and after their employment or engagement with the Company.

B. Non-Disclosure Agreements

The Company may require employees, contractors, and other personnel to enter into non-disclosure agreements or other confidentiality agreements to protect the Company's Intellectual Property and Confidential Information.

C. Handling of Confidential Information

Employees, contractors, and other personnel must exercise reasonable care and diligence in handling and protecting the Company's Confidential Information, including but not limited to:

1. Limiting access to Confidential Information on a need-to-know basis.
2. Refraining from discussing or disclosing Confidential Information in public places or with unauthorized individuals.
3. Securely storing and transmitting Confidential Information.
4. Promptly reporting any suspected or actual breaches of confidentiality to the appropriate personnel.

V. Licensing

A. Inbound Licensing

The Company may enter into agreements to license or otherwise acquire rights to use Intellectual Property owned by third parties, subject to appropriate due diligence and approval processes.

B. Outbound Licensing

The Company may license or otherwise grant rights to use its Intellectual Property to third parties, subject to appropriate due diligence, approval processes, and terms and conditions that protect the Company's interests and rights.

C. Approval and Oversight

All licensing agreements and transactions involving the Company's Intellectual Property shall be subject to review and approval by the Legal Department and the appropriate business units or stakeholders within the Company.

VI. Conclusion

Compliance with this Policy is mandatory for all employees, contractors, and other personnel associated with the Company. Failure to comply with this Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or engagement, and potential legal action.

The Company reserves the right to modify or update this Policy at any time, as deemed necessary to protect its interests and comply with applicable laws and regulations.
