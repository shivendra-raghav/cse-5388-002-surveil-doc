Travel Policy

Introduction
At Acme Corporation, we recognize the importance of business travel in achieving our goals and maintaining strong relationships with our clients, partners, and colleagues. This travel policy outlines the guidelines and procedures that employees must follow when booking and undertaking business travel. The policy is designed to ensure that all travel-related expenses are reasonable, appropriate, and aligned with the company's financial and operational objectives.

The provisions of this policy apply to all employees, regardless of their position or level within the organization. Failure to adhere to the guidelines set forth in this document may result in the denial of reimbursement or disciplinary action. If you have any questions or need clarification on any aspect of the travel policy, please contact the Travel Management team.

Travel Booking
All business travel must be booked through the company's approved travel management provider, TravelPro. This ensures that travelers can take advantage of negotiated rates and discounts, as well as access 24/7 support and assistance during their trips.

When booking travel, employees must consider the most cost-effective options that meet the business needs. This includes selecting the lowest available fare for flights, choosing the most economical rental car or public transportation options, and finding reasonably priced accommodations. Employees are expected to book travel arrangements as far in advance as possible to take advantage of lower rates and availability.

Accommodation
Employees are expected to choose hotel accommodations that are reasonable and appropriate for the purpose of the trip. When possible, travelers should select properties that are within a 15-mile radius of the primary business location or event. Exceptions may be granted for conferences or meetings that are held at a specific hotel.

The maximum allowable nightly rate for hotel accommodations is $250 USD, excluding taxes and fees. If the preferred hotel is more expensive, the employee must obtain prior approval from their manager and the Travel Management team. In such cases, the employee may be responsible for the difference in cost.

Employees are encouraged to utilize the company's preferred hotel partners, which offer negotiated rates and amenities. A list of these properties can be found on the company's travel portal. If an employee chooses to stay at a non-preferred hotel, they must provide a justification for the decision and obtain approval from their manager.

Transportation
Air Travel
When booking air travel, employees should choose the most direct and economical route available. This includes considering factors such as flight duration, layover times, and overall cost. Whenever possible, employees should book economy class tickets, unless a business-class upgrade is approved by the employee's manager and the Travel Management team.

Employees are expected to book flights at least 14 days in advance to take advantage of lower fares. Last-minute bookings should be avoided except in cases of emergency or unavoidable circumstances.

Ground Transportation
For local transportation during business trips, employees should consider the following options:
- Ride-sharing services (e.g., Uber, Lyft)
- Public transportation (e.g., bus, subway, train)
- Rental cars
- Taxis

Employees should select the most cost-effective option that meets their transportation needs. Rental cars should be reserved through the company's preferred vendors to ensure access to discounted rates and insurance coverage.

When renting a vehicle, employees must follow all applicable laws and regulations, including the use of seatbelts, adherence to speed limits, and refraining from distracted or impaired driving. Employees are responsible for any parking tickets, traffic violations, or damage incurred during the rental period.

Personal Vehicles
The use of personal vehicles for business travel is allowed, but employees must obtain prior approval from their manager. Reimbursement for mileage will be provided at the current standard mileage rate set by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). Employees must maintain valid driver's licenses and appropriate insurance coverage.

Expense Reporting
All business travel expenses must be submitted for reimbursement through the company's expense reporting system. Employees are required to provide detailed receipts and documentation for all expenses, including the purpose of the trip and any relevant business context.

The following expenses are generally considered reimbursable under the company's travel policy:
- Airfare
- Hotel accommodations
- Ground transportation (e.g., ride-sharing, public transit, rental cars)
- Meals (subject to per diem limits)
- Necessary business-related incidentals (e.g., internet access, business supplies)

Employees must use the company-issued credit card <CREDIT_CARD_NUMBER> for all travel-related purchases. Personal credit cards should only be used in exceptional circumstances, and the employee must provide a justification for the use of a personal card.

Meal expenses are reimbursed up to a maximum of $75 USD per day, inclusive of tax and tip. Employees are expected to exercise reasonable judgment and discretion when dining out for business purposes. Alcohol consumption should be limited and is only reimbursable in moderation.

Employees must submit their expense reports within 14 days of the completion of the trip. Late submissions may be subject to delayed reimbursement or non-payment. If an employee is unsure about the reimbursability of a particular expense, they should consult with the Travel Management team at prior to incurring the cost.

Conclusion
Adherence to this travel policy is crucial for ensuring the responsible and efficient use of the company's resources. Employees are expected to exercise good judgment, prioritize cost-effectiveness, and maintain appropriate documentation for all business travel-related expenses.

By following the guidelines outlined in this policy, employees can contribute to the company's overall financial well-being and support its strategic objectives through effective and responsible business travel.
