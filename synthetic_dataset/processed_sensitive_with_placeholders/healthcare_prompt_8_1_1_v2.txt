Coverage Details

Your Sunshine Health Solutions plan provides comprehensive coverage for a wide range of healthcare services, including:

- Annual wellness visits and preventive care: This includes routine check-ups, immunizations, and cancer screenings.
- Inpatient and outpatient hospital care: Your plan covers medically necessary hospital stays, surgeries, and treatments.
- Prescription drug coverage: You have access to a broad network of participating pharmacies, and your plan covers a variety of both generic and brand-name medications.
- Mental health and substance abuse services: Your plan includes coverage for counseling, therapy, and treatment for mental health conditions and substance abuse disorders.
- Emergency and urgent care: In the event of a medical emergency or urgent care need, your plan provides coverage for necessary treatment, both in-network and out-of-network.

It's important to note that your specific coverage details may vary based on the plan you have selected. You can review the full details of your coverage by accessing your member portal or contacting our customer service team.

Limitations

While your Sunshine Health Solutions plan provides comprehensive coverage, there are some limitations and exclusions to be aware of:

- Pre-existing conditions: Your plan may have a waiting period or exclusions for pre-existing medical conditions, so it's essential to review your policy details carefully.
- Out-of-network providers: If you choose to receive care from a provider that is not part of our network, you may be responsible for a higher portion of the costs or even the full cost of the services.
- Cosmetic procedures: Your plan generally does not cover elective cosmetic procedures, such as certain plastic surgeries, unless they are deemed medically necessary.
- Experimental or investigational treatments: Your plan will not cover treatments that are considered experimental or investigational, as they have not been proven safe and effective.

It's important to review your plan's specific limitations and exclusions to ensure that you understand what is and is not covered. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our customer service team.
