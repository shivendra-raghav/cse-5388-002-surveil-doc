Gift and Entertainment Policy

Introduction
At Acme Incorporated, we are committed to conducting business with the highest standards of integrity and ethics. Our reputation is one of our most valuable assets, and it is essential that we maintain the trust and confidence of our customers, partners, and the communities in which we operate. This Gift and Entertainment Policy ("Policy") outlines the guidelines and procedures for the acceptance and provision of gifts, entertainment, and other business courtesies, in alignment with our Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy.

The purpose of this Policy is to ensure that all employees, officers, and directors of Acme Incorporated ("Covered Persons") understand their responsibilities and obligations regarding the offering and receiving of gifts, entertainment, and other business courtesies. By adhering to this Policy, we aim to prevent conflicts of interest, maintain transparency, and comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), the UK Bribery Act, and other anti-bribery and corruption laws.

Acceptable Gifts and Entertainment
Gifts and entertainment can be appropriate ways to build and maintain business relationships, as long as they are reasonable, proportionate, and comply with this Policy. Covered Persons may offer or accept gifts and entertainment, provided that they meet the following criteria:

1. Legitimacy: The gift or entertainment must be directly related to a legitimate business purpose, such as the promotion, demonstration, or explanation of Acme Incorporated's products or services, or the execution or performance of a contract.

2. Transparency: The gift or entertainment must be openly and transparently provided, with no attempt to conceal or misrepresent its nature or purpose.

3. Proportionality: The value of the gift or entertainment must be reasonable and proportionate to the business relationship and local customs. Extravagant or lavish gifts and entertainment are prohibited.

4. Legality: The gift or entertainment must comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards in the jurisdictions where Acme Incorporated operates.

5. Reciprocity: Gifts and entertainment should be offered and accepted in a reciprocal manner, with no expectation of special treatment or undue influence.

Approval Process
Covered Persons must obtain prior written approval from their direct supervisor and the Compliance Department before offering or accepting any gift or entertainment that exceeds the value of $100 (or local currency equivalent). The Compliance Department can be reached at compliance@acmeinc.com or by mail at 123 Main Street, Cityville, CA 12345.

The approval request must include the following information:
- The name and title of the recipient or provider of the gift or entertainment
- A description of the gift or entertainment, including its estimated value
- The business purpose for the gift or entertainment
- The frequency and total value of gifts or entertainment provided to or received from the same individual or organization within the past 12 months
- Deposit the fee to <BANK_ACCOUNT_NUMBER> and provide the receipt number.
The Compliance Department will review the request and provide a decision within five (5) business days. Approved gifts and entertainment must be properly recorded in the Company's books and records.

Recordkeeping
Covered Persons must maintain accurate and complete records of all gifts and entertainment provided or received, regardless of value. This includes the following information:
- Date of the gift or entertainment
- Name and title of the recipient or provider
- Description of the gift or entertainment
- Estimated value of the gift or entertainment
- Business purpose
- Approval documentation, if applicable

These records must be submitted to the Compliance Department on a quarterly basis or upon request.

Violations and Penalties
Violations of this Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Covered Persons who engage in bribery, corruption, or other unethical conduct may also face civil or criminal penalties under applicable laws.

If a Covered Person becomes aware of any potential violation of this Policy, they must report it immediately to the Compliance Department at compliance@acmeinc.com or by mail at 123 Main Street, Cityville, CA 12345. Acme Incorporated strictly prohibits retaliation against any individual who reports a suspected violation in good faith.

Conclusion
By adhering to this Gift and Entertainment Policy, Covered Persons at Acme Incorporated can maintain the highest standards of integrity and ethical conduct, while building and strengthening valuable business relationships. If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Policy, please contact the Compliance Department.
