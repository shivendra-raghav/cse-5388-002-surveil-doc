# IT Security Policy Documentation

## Introduction

At ABC Corp, we take the security and protection of our systems and data very seriously. This IT Security Policy outlines the guidelines and procedures that all employees must follow to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our information assets. By adhering to these policies, we can mitigate the risk of cyber threats, unauthorized access, and data breaches, thereby safeguarding the organization's critical information and maintaining the trust of our clients and stakeholders.

## Access Control

Proper access control is a fundamental component of our IT security strategy. All employees are required to have a unique user account with a strong password to access our systems and applications. The credentials for the IT Administrator account are as follows:

- **Username:** <USERNAME>
- **Password:** <PASSWORD>

This IT Administrator account is used to access our ticketing system, such as JIRA or ServiceNow, to manage incidents, track IT support requests, and maintain the overall health of our IT infrastructure.

Access to sensitive information and critical systems is restricted based on the principle of least privilege. Employees are granted the minimum level of access required to perform their job duties. Managers and department heads are responsible for regularly reviewing and updating user access permissions to ensure that they align with the current job responsibilities and business needs.

All access attempts are logged, and any suspicious activity is closely monitored by our security team. Employees are prohibited from sharing their login credentials or allowing unauthorized individuals to access our systems. Failure to comply with these access control policies may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

## Password Management

Strong password management is essential to protect our systems and data from unauthorized access. All employees are required to create and maintain complex passwords that meet the following criteria:

- Minimum length of 12 characters
- Combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters
- No common words, phrases, or personal information
- Unique for each account and not reused across multiple systems

Employees must change their passwords every 90 days, and they are not permitted to reuse the same password for a minimum of 12 months. Password sharing is strictly prohibited, and employees are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials at all times.

In the event of a suspected password breach or compromise, employees must immediately report the incident to the IT security team. The team will then initiate the appropriate incident response procedures, which may include resetting passwords, monitoring for unauthorized access, and implementing additional security measures as necessary.

## Incident Response

Our organization has a robust incident response plan in place to address security incidents and minimize their impact on our operations. All employees are responsible for promptly reporting any suspected security breaches, malware infections, or other suspicious activities to the IT security team.

The incident response team, led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), John Doe, will investigate the incident, contain the threat, and coordinate the recovery efforts. Depending on the severity of the incident, the team may also involve external security experts, legal counsel, and regulatory authorities as needed.

In the event of a data breach or unauthorized access to sensitive information, the incident response team will follow the appropriate notification procedures, including informing affected individuals, clients, and regulatory bodies as required by law.

## Handling of Access Tokens

Many of our systems and applications rely on API integrations to facilitate data exchange and streamline business processes. To secure these integrations, we utilize access tokens that serve as temporary credentials for authorized access.

Employees who are responsible for managing API integrations must ensure the proper handling of access tokens. This includes:

- Generating unique access tokens for each integration
- Storing access tokens securely, such as in a password manager or encrypted database
- Regularly reviewing and rotating access tokens to minimize the risk of exposure
- Revoking access tokens immediately upon the termination of a contract or the completion of a project
- Avoiding the inclusion of access tokens in code repositories, configuration files, or other locations where they may be accidentally exposed

Improper handling of access tokens can lead to unauthorized access, data breaches, and other security incidents. Employees who fail to follow the established procedures for access token management may face disciplinary action.

## Conclusion

The IT Security Policy outlined in this document is designed to protect our organization's critical information assets and ensure the continued operation of our business. All employees are required to familiarize themselves with these policies and adhere to the guidelines and procedures outlined herein.

Failure to comply with the IT Security Policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment. Employees are encouraged to report any suspected security incidents or policy violations to the IT security team immediately.

By working together to uphold these security standards, we can maintain the trust of our clients, safeguard our competitive advantage, and demonstrate our commitment to responsible data stewardship.
