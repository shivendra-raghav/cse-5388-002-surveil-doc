Introduction:
Welcome to Pixel Perfect Designs! We are thrilled to have you on board as our newest team member. This onboarding documentation will guide you through the necessary steps to get set up with our design software, project management tools, and finance department procedures. Please follow the instructions carefully and do not hesitate to reach out to your manager or the IT support team if you encounter any issues.

Design Software Installation:
At Pixel Perfect Designs, we use Adobe Creative Cloud as our primary design tool. To install the software on your work computer, follow these steps:

1. Visit adobe.com and click on the "Download" button.
2. Select the appropriate version compatible with your operating system.
3. Run the downloaded installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
4. Once the installation is complete, launch the software.
5. You will be prompted to enter your <USERNAME> and <PASSWORD>. These credentials will be provided to you by our IT department.
6. Ensure that you keep your <PASSWORD> confidential and do not share it with anyone.

Note: If you encounter any issues during the installation process, please contact our IT support team at itsupport@pixelperfectdesigns.com.

Project Management Tool Access:
To collaborate effectively with our team and manage projects efficiently, we use Asana. Here's how to gain access:

1. Visit asana.com.
2. Click on the "Sign Up" or "Create Account" button.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to create your account, using your Pixel Perfect Designs email address.
4. Once your account is created, you will receive an invitation to join the Pixel Perfect Designs workspace.
5. Accept the invitation, and you will have full access to our projects, tasks, and communication channels.
