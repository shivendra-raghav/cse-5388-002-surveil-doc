Introduction:

Welcome to Pinnacle Health Insurance, where we prioritize your well-being and provide comprehensive coverage tailored to your healthcare needs. This document outlines the key features, benefits, and resources available to you as a valued member of our insurance plan.

At Pinnacle Health Insurance, we understand the importance of accessible and affordable healthcare. Our mission is to empower you with the knowledge and resources necessary to make informed decisions about your health and wellness journey.

Covered Benefits:

Pinnacle Health Insurance offers a wide range of covered benefits to ensure you receive the care you deserve. Our plan includes, but is not limited to:

1. Preventive Care Services: Annual check-ups, routine screenings, and immunizations are fully covered to promote proactive health management.

2. Inpatient and Outpatient Hospital Care: Whether you require hospitalization or outpatient treatment, our plan covers essential medical services, including surgeries, diagnostic tests, and treatments.

3. Prescription Drug Coverage: Access to a comprehensive formulary of prescription medications, including generic and brand-name drugs, is provided to manage chronic conditions and acute illnesses effectively.

4. Mental Health and Substance Abuse Treatment: Recognizing the importance of mental well-being, our plan covers mental health counseling, therapy sessions, and substance abuse rehabilitation programs.

5. Maternity and Newborn Care: Expectant mothers and newborns receive comprehensive prenatal, delivery, and postpartum care, ensuring a healthy start for the newest members of your family.

Out-of-Network Coverage:

While we encourage the use of our extensive network of healthcare providers, we understand that circumstances may arise where out-of-network care is necessary. In such cases, Pinnacle Health Insurance provides partial coverage for eligible services, subject to deductibles and coinsurance. Please refer to your plan documents for specific details on out-of-network coverage and reimbursement processes.

Member Resources:

Pinnacle Health Insurance is committed to supporting your healthcare journey by providing valuable resources and tools. Our member portal offers convenient access to the following:

1. Online Account Management: Easily manage your policy, update personal information, and view claim status and Explanation of Benefits (EOB) statements.

2. Provider Directory: Search for in-network healthcare providers, including doctors, hospitals, and specialists, based on your location and medical needs.

3. Health and Wellness Programs: Access to a variety of educational materials, fitness trackers, and preventive care initiatives to promote a healthy lifestyle.

4. 24/7 Nurse Hotline: Speak with a registered nurse anytime, day or night, to address non-emergency medical concerns and receive guidance on appropriate care options.
