With OnboardMe, you can optimize your onboarding experience, ensuring a smooth and engaging journey for every new team member.

Installation
To get started with OnboardMe, follow these simple steps:

Mobile App Installation (Android and iOS):

1. Open the Google Play Store or Apple App Store on your mobile device.
2. Search for "OnboardMe" in the search bar.
3. Locate the official OnboardMe app and tap "Install" or "Get."
4. Once the installation is complete, you can launch the app from your device's home screen or app drawer.

Web Application:

1. Open your preferred web browser.
2. Navigate to https://www.onboardme.com.
3. The OnboardMe web application will load, providing you with access to all its features and functionalities.

Employee Details
OnboardMe streamlines the process of gathering essential employee information, ensuring a comprehensive and organized onboarding experience. Here's how you can access and update your employee details:

1. Launch the OnboardMe application (mobile or web).
2. Log in using your assigned <USERNAME> and <PASSWORD>.
3. Navigate to the "Employee Profile" section.
4. Review and update your personal information, including contact details, emergency contacts, and any other relevant data.
5. Be sure to save your changes before exiting the section.

Note: It is crucial to keep your login credentials (<USERNAME> and <PASSWORD>) secure and confidential to maintain the integrity of your account and protect sensitive information.
