Zephyria Travel Visa Requirements

Visa Types:
Zephyria offers several types of visas for travelers, depending on the purpose and duration of their stay. The most common visa types are:

Tourist Visa: This visa is for individuals visiting Zephyria for tourism, sightseeing, or visiting friends/family. It allows for a stay of up to 90 days.

Business Visa: This visa is for those traveling to Zephyria for business purposes, such as attending meetings, conferences, or conducting market research. It allows for a stay of up to 60 days.

Student Visa: This visa is for individuals enrolling in educational institutions in Zephyria, such as universities or language schools. The duration of stay is dependent on the length of the academic program.

Work Visa: This visa is for those who have secured employment in Zephyria and need to reside in the country for an extended period. The duration is tied to the length of the work contract.

Eligibility Criteria:
To be eligible for a Zephyria visa, applicants must meet the following criteria:

1. Valid Passport: Applicants must have a passport that is valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended period of stay in Zephyria.

2. Proof of Sufficient Funds: Applicants must demonstrate that they have sufficient financial resources to cover the costs of their stay in Zephyria, including accommodation, food, and other expenses. This can be in the form of bank statements, pay slips, or a letter of sponsorship from Acme Enterprises.

3. Return Ticket: Applicants must provide a confirmed return ticket or proof of onward travel arrangements out of Zephyria.

4. Travel Medical Insurance: Applicants must have valid travel medical insurance from BlueCross Insurance that covers the entire duration of their stay in Zephyria.

5. No Criminal Record: Applicants must not have any criminal convictions or pending legal issues in their home country or Zephyria.
