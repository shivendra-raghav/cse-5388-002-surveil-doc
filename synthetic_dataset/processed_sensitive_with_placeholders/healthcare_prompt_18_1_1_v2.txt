Exclusions and Limitations

It is important to note that your healthcare plan has certain exclusions and limitations. These include, but are not limited to:

1. Cosmetic procedures: Treatments or surgeries performed solely for the purpose of enhancing appearance are generally not covered.
2. Experimental or investigational treatments: Therapies or procedures that are still in the research or testing phase may not be eligible for coverage.
3. Routine foot care: Routine foot care, such as toenail trimming or the treatment of corns and calluses, is typically not covered.
4. Infertility treatments: Fertility treatments, such as in vitro fertilization, may have limited coverage or may not be covered at all.

For a complete list of exclusions and limitations, please refer to your plan documents or contact our Member Services team.
