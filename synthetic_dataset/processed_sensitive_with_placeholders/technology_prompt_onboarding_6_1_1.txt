## AWS Access Information

To access our AWS environment, you will be provided with an Individual AWS User account. This account will have specific permissions and access levels tailored to your role within the company.

1. **IAM User Creation**: Your IAM (Identity and Access Management) user account will be created by the AWS Administrator. You will receive an email with your login credentials, including your <USERNAME> and a temporary <PASSWORD>.

2. **Initial Login**: Visit the AWS Management Console (https://console.aws.amazon.com) and enter your <USERNAME> and the temporary <PASSWORD> provided to you.

3. **Password Reset**: Upon your initial login, you will be prompted to reset your password. Please choose a secure password and keep it confidential.

4. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: For enhanced security, you will be required to set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) for your account. Follow the prompts to configure MFA using a virtual or hardware-based authentication app.

5. **Access Policies**: Your account will have specific access policies applied based on your role and responsibilities within the company. These policies will determine the AWS services and resources you can access and manage.
