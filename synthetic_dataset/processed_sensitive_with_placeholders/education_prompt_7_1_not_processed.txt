# Lincoln High School Parent Newsletter

## Upcoming Events

### Spring Music Showcase
Join us on Saturday, May 13th at 7:00 PM for our annual Spring Music Showcase. This event celebrates the incredible musical talents of our students, featuring performances by the school band, choir, orchestra, and various student soloists and ensembles. Tickets are $5 for adults and $3 for students, and can be purchased online or at the door.

### Prom 2024
This year's prom theme is "A Night Under the Stars" and will take place on Saturday, May 20th from 7:00 PM to 11:00 PM at the Acme Hotel Ballroom. Tickets are $50 per person and can be purchased through the school's online payment portal. The deadline to purchase tickets is May 10th. 

### Graduation Ceremony
Lincoln High School's Class of 2024 Graduation Ceremony will be held on Saturday, June 3rd at 10:00 AM in the school auditorium. Each graduate will receive 4 tickets for family members. Additional tickets can be requested by contacting the school office using the password <PASSWORD>.

## Attendance Reminders
As the school year winds down, it's crucial that students maintain regular attendance. Please remember to report any absences or tardies to the attendance office as soon as possible. Excessive absences may result in loss of credit or other disciplinary actions.

## PTA Updates
The Lincoln High School PTA would like to thank all the parents and guardians who have volunteered their time and resources this year. Your contributions have made a significant impact on our school community. 

If you are interested in getting more involved with the PTA, please consider attending our next meeting on May 15th at 6:30 PM in the school library. We are always looking for new members and ideas to enhance the student experience.

## Contact Information
If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the appropriate school staff members. You can find their contact information on the Lincoln High School website.

We value your partnership and look forward to a successful end to the school year.

The Lincoln High School Administration
