Benefit Summary
Medical Coverage
Our healthcare plan offers a wide range of medical services to meet your healthcare needs. This includes coverage for:

- Annual wellness exams and preventive care
- Physician office visits for illness or injury
- Emergency room and urgent care services
- Inpatient hospital stays
- Diagnostic tests and lab work
- Rehabilitative therapy (physical, occupational, speech)
- Durable medical equipment (wheelchairs, crutches, etc.)

Your plan has a $1,500 deductible, and you are responsible for paying 20% of the costs for in-network services until you reach your out-of-pocket maximum of $5,000. Once you meet the out-of-pocket maximum, your plan will cover 100% of the costs for the remainder of the plan year.

Prescription Drug Coverage
Your healthcare plan includes prescription drug coverage. You will have access to our network of participating pharmacies, and your copays will vary based on the tier of the medication. Generic drugs have the lowest copays, while brand-name and specialty medications have higher copays.

You can manage your prescriptions through our online portal or mobile app, where you can refill medications, check drug costs, and find participating pharmacies near you.

Dental and Vision Coverage
In addition to medical coverage, your plan also includes dental and vision benefits. This includes coverage for routine cleanings, exams, and basic procedures. You also have an allowance of $200 for eyeglasses or contact lenses each year.

Limitations and Exclusions
It's important to understand that not all medical services are covered under your healthcare plan. Here are some key limitations and exclusions:

- Cosmetic procedures (e.g., elective plastic surgery)
- Experimental or investigational treatments
- Infertility treatments
- Long-term custodial care
- Non-emergency care received outside of the plan's network
- Routine foot care (unless you have a qualifying medical condition)

Additionally, there are annual limits on certain benefits, such as a maximum of 30 physical therapy visits or a limit of $1,000 covered for durable medical equipment.

Please refer to your full plan documents for a complete list of limitations and exclusions.
