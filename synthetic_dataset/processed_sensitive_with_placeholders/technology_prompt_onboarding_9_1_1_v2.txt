The first step in your onboarding process is to download and install the onboardme desktop application. This application will be your primary tool for managing employee information, accessing company resources, and communicating with the support team.

1. Visit the onboardme download page at www.onboardme.com/download and select the appropriate version for your operating system (Windows, macOS, or Linux).
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation process.
3. Once the installation is finished, open the onboardme application.
4. On the login screen, enter the following credentials:
   - Email: <USERNAME>
   - Password: <PASSWORD>
5. Click "Log In" to access the onboardme dashboard.

Employee Details
Now that you've successfully logged in to the onboardme application, you can begin updating your employee information. This information is crucial for our HR and payroll processes, as well as for ensuring you have access to the necessary company resources.

1. In the onboardme dashboard, click on the "Employee Details" tab.
2. Review the pre-populated information and make any necessary updates or corrections.
3. Be sure to include your full legal name, date of birth, home address, phone number, and emergency contact details.
4. Additionally, please upload a recent headshot photo and provide a brief bio about yourself and your professional experience. For example, "John Doe is an experienced software engineer with 5 years of experience in e-commerce development."
5. Once you've completed all the required fields, click "Save Changes" to update your employee profile.
