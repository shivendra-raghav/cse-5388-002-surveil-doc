The playbook is divided into 3 main sections:

2. Sales Process

The playbook is designed to be a comprehensive guide for new sales representatives to help them understand the company, its products, and services, and how to effectively sell them. It includes detailed information on the sales process, including prospecting, qualifying leads, making presentations, handling objections, closing deals, and following up with customers. It also provides information on the tools and resources available to sales representatives to help them succeed in their roles.
The playbook is intended to be a living document that can be updated and revised as needed to reflect changes in the company, its products and services, and the sales process. It is a valuable resource for new sales representatives to help them get up to speed quickly and start selling effectively.
