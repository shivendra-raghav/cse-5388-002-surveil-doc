# Student Disciplinary Report

## Date: May 1, 2024
## Student: Jamie Rojas
## Grade: 10th

### Incident Summary
On April 20, 2024, student Jamie Rojas, SSN: <SSN>, was involved in an altercation with fellow student Ethan Patel during the lunch period in the school cafeteria. The incident was witnessed by faculty member Ms. Sarah Goldstein, the assigned cafeteria monitor.

According to the incident report, the altercation began when Jamie approached Ethan's table and the two students started arguing. The argument quickly escalated, with Jamie shoving Ethan and Ethan retaliating by throwing his lunch tray at Jamie. This resulted in food and debris being scattered across the cafeteria floor.

Ms. Goldstein immediately intervened, ordering the two students to stop the altercation. Jamie refused to comply and proceeded to shove Ms. Goldstein, causing her to fall backwards and hit her head on a nearby table. Campus security was promptly notified, and the two students were escorted to the principal's office.

Jamie Rojas was observed to have a minor scratch on his arm, presumably from the initial shove. Ms. Goldstein reported feeling dizzy and experiencing a mild headache following the incident. Ethan Patel did not appear to sustain any physical injuries.

### Disciplinary Action
Per Lincoln High School's code of conduct, both Jamie Rojas and Ethan Patel have been suspended from school for 3 days. Jamie Rojas will face additional disciplinary measures for assaulting a faculty member.

Ms. Goldstein has been advised to seek medical attention for her reported head injury. The school nurse will follow up with her condition.

This incident has been documented, and copies of the report will be placed in the student files of Jamie Rojas, SSN: <SSN>, and Ethan Patel, SSN: <SSN>. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school principal, Mrs. Emily Thompson, at <SSN>.edu.

### Reflection and Next Steps
Jamie Rojas's actions during this incident were unacceptable and in direct violation of the school's code of conduct. His refusal to follow the instructions of a faculty member and the physical altercation with both a student and a staff member are serious offenses that cannot be tolerated.

Moving forward, Jamie will be required to attend counseling sessions to address the underlying issues that led to this incident. The school will work closely with Jamie and his family to develop a behavior improvement plan and ensure he understands the importance of respecting authority, resolving conflicts peacefully, and maintaining a safe and conducive learning environment for all students.

Additionally, the school will review the existing policies and procedures related to campus supervision and emergency response to identify any areas for improvement. The safety and well-being of our students and staff are of the utmost importance, and we are committed to taking all necessary steps to prevent similar incidents from occurring in the future.

If you have any further questions or concerns regarding this disciplinary matter, please do not hesitate to contact the school administration.
