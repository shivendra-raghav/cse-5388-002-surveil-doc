   - Outpatient surgeries
   - Emergency room visits

   - Inpatient and outpatient services

7. Maternity Care:
   - Prenatal and postnatal care

Exclusions

While ABC Health's healthcare coverage plan aims to provide comprehensive benefits, there are certain services and treatments that are excluded from coverage. These exclusions include, but are not limited to:

1. Cosmetic procedures (unless medically necessary)
2. Experimental or investigational treatments
3. Infertility treatments
4. Long-term care services
5. Non-emergency services received outside the United States
6. Alternative therapies (e.g., acupuncture, homeopathy)

Please note that this is not an exhaustive list, and specific exclusions may vary based on your plan details. It is recommended to review your plan documents or consult with our Member Services team for a complete understanding of the exclusions applicable to your coverage.
