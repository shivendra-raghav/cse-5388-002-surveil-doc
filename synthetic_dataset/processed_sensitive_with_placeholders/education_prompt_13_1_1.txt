Title: Student Evaluation Report

Name: Sarah Johnson
Date of Birth: 05/12/2010
Grade: 5th
School: Maple Grove Elementary

Reason for Referral:
Sarah Johnson was referred for a special education evaluation due to concerns regarding reading comprehension and behavior issues. The referral was initiated by her classroom teacher.

Background Information:
Sarah Johnson is a 12-year-old female who resides with her parents and younger brother. She has a history of attention difficulties and struggles with following instructions. Her primary language is English.

Evaluation Process:
The evaluation process included the following assessments and procedures:
- Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children (WISC-V)
- Woodcock-Johnson Tests of Achievement (WJ-IV)
- Behavior Assessment System for Children (BASC-3)
- Classroom Observations
- Parent/Teacher Interviews

Evaluation Results:
Cognitive Ability: Sarah's overall cognitive ability falls within the Average range (Full Scale IQ = 98).

Academic Achievement: Sarah's reading comprehension skills are significantly below age and grade level expectations (Standard Score = 75). Her skills in other academic areas, such as mathematics and written expression, are within the Average range.

Behavior/Social-Emotional: Rating scales and observations indicate significant concerns with inattentive behavior, impulsivity, and difficulty following classroom rules and routines.

Other Relevant Findings: No significant concerns noted in areas of speech/language, motor skills, or adaptive functioning.

Determination of Eligibility:
Based on the evaluation results, Sarah Johnson meets the criteria for special education services under the eligibility category of Specific Learning Disability (with a deficit in Reading Comprehension) and Other Health Impairment (due to Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder). She exhibits significant deficits in reading comprehension and attention/behavior regulation that adversely impact her educational performance.

Recommendations:
To address Sarah's needs, the following accommodations and services are recommended:
- Extended time on tests
- Preferential seating near the instructor
- Resource Room support for Reading/Language Arts
- Occupational Therapy consultation for attention strategies
- School Counseling Services for behavior intervention

It is also recommended that Sarah's progress be monitored regularly, and the Individualized Education Program (IEP) be reviewed and updated annually or as needed.

Additional Notes:
Sarah's parents express concern about her frustration with academic tasks and desire for her to receive appropriate support. Sarah's strengths include her creative thinking abilities and strong interpersonal skills.
