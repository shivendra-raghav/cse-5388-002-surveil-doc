Acme Corporation Gift and Entertainment Policy

1. Introduction
Acme Corporation (the "Company") is committed to maintaining high ethical standards and complying with all applicable laws and regulations governing gifts and entertainment. This Gift and Entertainment Policy (the "Policy") provides guidance on the appropriate giving and receiving of gifts, meals, and entertainment to or from current or potential business associates.

2. Purpose
The purpose of this Policy is to ensure that the Company's business dealings are conducted in a fair and transparent manner, free from any actual or perceived conflicts of interest or improper influence. This Policy aims to prevent situations where the exchange of gifts or entertainment could potentially compromise the objectivity of business decisions or create a sense of obligation.

3. Scope
This Policy applies to all employees, officers, directors, and agents of the Company, as well as their immediate family members, when engaging in business activities on behalf of the Company.

4. Definitions
4.1. Gift: Anything of value given or received without expectation of compensation or return, including but not limited to cash, gift cards, vouchers, goods, services, discounts, or favors.
4.2. Entertainment: Any activity or event, such as meals, drinks, recreation, or hospitality, where the provider is present and hosting the recipient.

5. General Principles
5.1. Employees must exercise good judgment and moderation when giving or receiving gifts and entertainment.
5.2. Gifts and entertainment must be lawful, reasonable, and in line with generally accepted business practices.
5.3. Gifts and entertainment must not be given or received with the intent to improperly influence business decisions or gain an unfair advantage.
5.4. Employees must obtain prior approval from their manager or the Compliance Department for gifts or entertainment exceeding [Approved Threshold Amount].

6. Restrictions and Prohibitions
6.1. The following types of gifts and entertainment are strictly prohibited:
    - Cash or cash equivalents (e.g., gift cards, vouchers)
    - Gifts or entertainment that are illegal or inappropriate
    - Gifts or entertainment that could be perceived as a bribe or kickback
    - Gifts or entertainment involving adult entertainment or similar venues
    - Gifts or entertainment that violate the policies of the recipient's organization

6.2. Employees must not accept or provide gifts or entertainment that could reasonably be expected to:
    - Influence business decisions
    - Create a sense of obligation or improper advantage
    - Cause embarrassment to the Company or the recipient

7. Reporting and Documentation
7.1. Employees must accurately record and report all gifts and entertainment given or received in the Company's Gift and Entertainment Register, maintained by the Compliance Department.
7.2. Supporting documentation, such as receipts or invoices, must be provided for all reported gifts and entertainment.
7.3. Employees must promptly report any suspected violations of this Policy to their manager or the Compliance Department.

8. Disciplinary Action
Failure to comply with this Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, in accordance with the Company's disciplinary procedures.

9. Policy Review and Updates
This Policy will be reviewed and updated periodically to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations, as well as alignment with industry best practices.
