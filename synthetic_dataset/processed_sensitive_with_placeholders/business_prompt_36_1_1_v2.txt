Introduction
At ABC Corporation, we are committed to maintaining a professional and respectful work environment where all employees are treated with dignity and respect. Harassment of any kind, whether based on race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, gender identity, and sexual orientation), national origin, age, disability, genetic information, or any other protected characteristic, will not be tolerated. This policy applies to all employees, including managers, supervisors, and non-supervisory staff, as well as to all individuals who interact with our employees, such as vendors, customers, and clients.

Definition of Harassment
Harassment is any unwelcome conduct that is based on a protected characteristic and that is severe or pervasive enough to create a work environment that a reasonable person would consider intimidating, hostile, or abusive. Harassment can take many forms, including but not limited to:

- Verbal harassment, such as offensive comments, slurs, or jokes related to a protected characteristic
- Physical harassment, such as unwanted touching, assault, or interference with an individual's movement or work
- Visual harassment, such as the display of offensive posters, symbols, cartoons, drawings, computer images, or emails
- Sexual harassment, which includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature

Prohibited Conduct
The following types of conduct are strictly prohibited under this policy:

- Verbal harassment, such as making derogatory comments, slurs, or jokes about an individual's race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or other protected characteristic
- Physical harassment, such as unwanted touching, assault, or interference with an individual's movement or work
- Visual harassment, such as displaying offensive posters, symbols, cartoons, drawings, computer images, or emails
- Sexual harassment, including unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature
- Retaliation against an individual who reports harassment or participates in an investigation of a harassment complaint

All reports of harassment or retaliation will be taken seriously and will be investigated promptly and thoroughly. Employees who report harassment or retaliation, or who participate in an investigation, will be protected from retaliation.

Investigation
When a report of harassment or retaliation is received, the Human Resources department will initiate a prompt and thorough investigation. The investigation may include interviews with the complainant, the accused, and any witnesses, as well as a review of relevant documents and other evidence.

The investigation will be conducted in a fair and impartial manner, and all information will be kept confidential to the extent possible. The complainant and the accused will be informed of the outcome of the investigation.

If the investigation determines that harassment or retaliation has occurred, appropriate disciplinary action will be taken, up to and including termination of employment. Disciplinary action may also be taken against any employee who fails to report harassment or retaliation, or who knowingly provides false information during an investigation.

Conclusion
At ABC Corporation, we are committed to maintaining a professional and respectful work environment that is free from harassment and retaliation. We encourage all employees to report any incidents of harassment or retaliation, and we will take prompt and appropriate action to address any such conduct.
