Activity Schedule
Your travel itinerary includes a variety of activities and experiences to ensure you make the most of your trip. Here is a detailed schedule of your daily activities:

Day 1 (June 5th): Arrival in New York City
- 2:30 PM - Arrive at JFK Airport and transfer to your hotel
- 4:00 PM - Check-in at Hilton Garden Inn Times Square
- 6:00 PM - Explore Times Square and have dinner at Carmine's Italian Restaurant

Day 2 (June 6th): Sightseeing in New York City
- 9:00 AM - Visit the 9/11 Memorial and Museum
- 12:00 PM - Enjoy a scenic cruise around the Statue of Liberty with Statue Cruises
- 3:00 PM - Explore Central Park and visit the American Museum of Natural History

Day 3 (June 7th): Brooklyn and Williamsburg
- 10:00 AM - Take the subway to Brooklyn and explore the vibrant neighborhood of Williamsburg
- 1:00 PM - Enjoy a walking tour of the Brooklyn Bridge with Free Tours by Foot
- 3:00 PM - Visit the Brooklyn Botanic Garden

Day 4 (June 8th): Day Trip to the Hamptons
- 8:00 AM - Depart for a day trip to the Hamptons with Hampton Jitney
- 10:00 AM - Explore the charming town of Sag Harbor
- 1:00 PM - Enjoy a scenic drive along the coast and visit the Coopers Beach
- 4:00 PM - Return to New York City

Day 5 (June 9th): Free Day in New York City
- Enjoy a free day to explore the city at your own pace
- Recommended activities: Visit the High Line, explore the West Village, and enjoy the vibrant nightlife at Comedy Cellar

Day 6 (June 10th): Depart for Niagara Falls
- 8:00 AM - Check out of your hotel in New York City
- 10:00 AM - Depart for Niagara Falls, Canada
- 3:00 PM - Arrive at Niagara Falls Marriott on the Falls and check-in

Day 7 (June 11th): Niagara Falls Exploration
- 9:00 AM - Experience the Hornblower Niagara Cruises boat tour at the base of the falls
- 11:00 AM - Visit the Niagara Falls Skylon Tower for panoramic views
- 1:00 PM - Explore the Niagara Parks Butterfly Conservatory
- 3:00 PM - Visit the Niagara-on-the-Lake region for wine tasting at Peller Estates Winery

Day 8 (June 12th): Niagara-on-the-Lake
- 10:00 AM - Check-in at the Pillar and Post Inn & Spa in Niagara-on-the-Lake
- 11:00 AM - Explore the charming streets and shop at the local boutiques on Queen Street
- 1:00 PM - Enjoy a horse-drawn carriage ride through the historic town with Sentineal Carriages
- 3:00 PM - Visit the Niagara-on-the-Lake Museum

Day 9 (June 13th): Leisure Day in Niagara-on-the-Lake
- Enjoy a leisurely day exploring the town at your own pace
- Recommended activities: Visit the Shaw Festival Theatre, relax at the hotel's 100 Fountain Spa, or take a scenic bicycle ride along the Niagara River Recreational Trail

Day 10 (June 14th): Departure
- 10:00 AM - Check out of your hotel in Niagara-on-the-Lake
- 11:00 AM - Depart for Buffalo Niagara International Airport
- 4:00 PM - Depart Buffalo for your flight back to Los Angeles
