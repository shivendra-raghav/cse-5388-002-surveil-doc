Subject: Redwood Valley Elementary School Newsletter - May 2023

Dear Redwood Valley Families,

We hope this newsletter finds you well as we approach the end of another fantastic school year. It has been a busy and eventful few months, and we are excited to share the latest updates from Redwood Valley Elementary.

School Events and Announcements

Spring Carnival Success
Our annual Spring Carnival was a huge success! We had an incredible turnout, and the students thoroughly enjoyed the games, rides, and delicious food. A big thank you to all the parent volunteers from the Redwood Valley PTA who made this event possible. Your dedication and hard work are truly appreciated.

Field Day Fun
Our annual Field Day was a resounding success! Students from all grade levels participated in a variety of fun and engaging activities, including sack races, relay races, and tug-of-war. The smiles and laughter on the students' faces were a testament to the hard work of our physical education team led by Coach Emily Thompson and the enthusiasm of our school community.

Congratulations to our Graduating Class
We are so proud of our fifth-grade students who are preparing to embark on their next academic journey at Oak Valley Middle School. Their hard work, dedication, and resilience have been an inspiration to us all. We wish them all the best as they move on to middle school. We will miss you, but we know you are ready for this next chapter.

Upcoming Events

Science Fair
Mark your calendars! Our annual Science Fair is just around the corner on May 20th. Students have been working diligently on their projects under the guidance of our science teacher, Mr. James Smith, and we can't wait to see their innovative ideas. Parents are invited to attend and support our budding scientists. The event will be held in the school gymnasium from 6:00 pm to 8:00 pm.

Summer Reading Challenge
This year, we are excited to launch our Summer Reading Challenge in partnership with the Redwood Valley Public Library. Students are encouraged to read as many books as possible over the summer break. Prizes and incentives, including gift cards to the local bookstore, will be awarded to those who meet their reading goals. Detailed information will be sent home with students in the coming weeks.

School Reminders
