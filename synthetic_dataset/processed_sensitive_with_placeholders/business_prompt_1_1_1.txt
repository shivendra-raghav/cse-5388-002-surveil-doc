### Employee Access

## Software Setup

Acme Solutions provides a range of software applications to support various business functions. The IT team is responsible for ensuring that these applications are properly installed, configured, and maintained across the organization.

### Standard Software Suite

All employees are provided with a standard software suite, including an operating system (Windows 10 Professional), productivity tools (Microsoft Office 365), and communication software (Microsoft Teams, Outlook).

To install the standard software suite, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the company's software deployment portal (Software Center) using your username and password.
2. Select the desired software package based on your department and role (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Finance).
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to initiate the installation process.

If you encounter any issues during the installation or have specific software requirements, please submit a ticket to the IT helpdesk.

## Troubleshooting

In the event of technical issues or system failures, the IT team is available to provide prompt support and resolution. Here are some common troubleshooting steps:

1. Check the IT knowledge base (IT Self-Service Portal) for solutions to known issues and frequently asked questions.
2. If the issue persists, submit a ticket to the IT helpdesk (via ServiceDesk Plus), providing detailed information about the problem, including error messages, screenshots, and any relevant logs.
3. An IT support technician will be assigned to your ticket and will work with you to diagnose and resolve the issue.

If the issue is critical or affects multiple users, escalate the ticket to ensure priority handling.
