Introduction
Welcome to Acme Corporation, a leading provider of innovative solutions in the technology industry. This handbook outlines our company policies, benefits, and expectations for all employees. Our goal is to foster a positive and productive work environment where everyone can thrive.

Employment Policies
Equal Opportunity Employer: Acme Corporation is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate based on race, color, religion, gender, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected status.

Probationary Period: All new employees are subject to a 90-day probationary period, during which their performance and suitability for the position will be evaluated.

Working Hours: Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible scheduling may be available for certain positions with manager approval.

Benefits
Health Insurance: Acme Corporation offers comprehensive health insurance plans for employees and their eligible dependents, including medical, dental, and vision coverage.

Paid Time Off: Employees are entitled to paid vacation days, sick leave, and holidays according to the company's PTO policy.

Retirement Plan: Acme Corporation provides a 401(k) retirement plan with an employer matching contribution up to 5% of an employee's salary.

Code of Conduct
Confidentiality: Employees must maintain the confidentiality of company information, including trade secrets, customer data, and proprietary information.

Conflict of Interest: Employees must avoid situations that create an actual or perceived conflict of interest between their personal interests and the company's interests.

Professional Conduct: Employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and treat all colleagues, customers, and business partners with respect and courtesy.

Conclusion
This employee handbook outlines the key policies and expectations at Acme Corporation.
