I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to provide an update on the ECAR and MAIN power markets from this past weekend.

ECAR:
There was ample generation available in ECAR over the weekend. FE offered 150 MW at $38 for a 16-hour schedule on Monday, but there was no interest at that price level. On Saturday, we had to move ECAR power down to Florida for most of the day due to TLRs being called. On Sunday evening during the peak, SOCO was on the bid at $25, but TLRs prevented any energy from moving into SERC. Most utilities in ECAR will likely be sellers in the morning hours.

The 16-hour average prices in ECAR were:
Friday: Bid $25.6, Offer $27.5
Saturday: Bid $10.5, Offer $26.9
Sunday: Bid $9.9, Offer $17.2

MAIN:
TLRs were prevalent over the weekend in MAIN, preventing CE, Ameren, and other MAIN members from sending power into SERC or FRCC. There were no legitimate bids, while sellers had ample power available. Ameren was effectively an island due to the TLRs. CE will be selling hourly on Monday, quoting an offer of $36 for a 16-hour schedule and $12 for the 7-hour block, but there were no bids to move that power. Ameren will also be selling hourly if TLRs do not kick in again.

The 16-hour average prices in MAIN were:
Friday: Bid $19.7, Offer $30.4
Saturday: Bid $10.1, Offer $20.7
Sunday: Bid $9.4, Offer $15.7
