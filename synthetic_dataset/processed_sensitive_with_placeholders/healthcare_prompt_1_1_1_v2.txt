Important Notices

As a new member of our healthcare plan, there are a few important notices you should be aware of:

1. Preventive Care Coverage: Our plans place a strong emphasis on preventive care, which means that certain routine screenings, immunizations, and wellness visits are covered at 100% with no out-of-pocket costs to you. We encourage you to take advantage of these preventive services to maintain your overall health and well-being.

2. In-Network Providers: To receive the highest level of coverage and minimize your out-of-pocket expenses, it is important to use healthcare providers and facilities that are within our network. You can find a list of in-network providers on our website or by contacting our member services team.

3. Pre-authorization Requirements: Some medical services, such as non-emergency hospitalizations, certain diagnostic tests, and specialized treatments, may require prior authorization from our plan. Failure to obtain the necessary pre-authorization could result in reduced coverage or even a denial of the claim. If you're unsure about a particular service, please contact us for guidance.

4. Coordination of Benefits: If you or your dependents have coverage under multiple health plans, it is important to inform us so that we can coordinate benefits and ensure that you receive the maximum coverage available to you. Please provide us with information about any other insurance plans you or your family members may have.

5. Timely Claim Submission: To ensure prompt processing and payment of your claims, please submit all medical, dental, and vision claims within the timely filing period specified in your plan documents, which is typically 12 months from the date of service.

6. Privacy and Confidentiality: We take the privacy and confidentiality of your personal and health information very seriously. Our policies and procedures are designed to protect your data in accordance with all applicable laws and regulations. If you have any questions or concerns about the handling of your information, please don't hesitate to contact our privacy officer.
