2. Enter your <USERNAME> and <PASSWORD> provided by IT.
3. Upon your first login, you'll be prompted to set up your profile and preferences.
4. Familiarize yourself with the CRM dashboard, which provides an overview of your sales activities, leads, and opportunities.

Commission Payments:
As a sales representative, a portion of your compensation is tied to your sales performance and commission earnings. To ensure accurate and timely commission payments, please follow these steps:

1. After closing a deal, promptly submit the necessary documentation, including the customer contract and order details, to our finance department.
2. For commission calculation purposes, you'll need to provide your <SSN> (Social Security Number) to our finance team. This sensitive information will be handled securely and confidentially.
3. Commission payments are processed on a monthly basis, and you'll receive a detailed commission statement outlining your earnings.

Note: Please exercise caution when sharing sensitive personal information like your <SSN> and ensure it is only provided through secure channels to authorized personnel.
