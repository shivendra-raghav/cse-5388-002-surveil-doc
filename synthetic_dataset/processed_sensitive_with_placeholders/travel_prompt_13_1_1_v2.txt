Membership Tiers:
The Wanderlust Travel Rewards Program offers three distinct membership tiers, each with its own set of exclusive benefits and perks. As you earn and redeem points, you'll have the opportunity to advance through the tiers, unlocking a world of elevated travel experiences.

1. Blue Tier:
- Earn 2 points per $1 spent on eligible travel purchases
- Earn 1 point per $1 spent on all other eligible purchases
- Redeem points for air travel, hotel stays, car rentals, and more
- Access to 24/7 customer support

2. Gold Tier (requires 25,000 points earned annually):
- Earn 3 points per $1 spent on eligible travel purchases
- Earn 1.5 points per $1 spent on all other eligible purchases
- Enjoy complimentary room upgrades and late checkout at partner hotels like Marriott and Hyatt
- Access to airport lounge facilities with Priority Pass
- Priority customer support

3. Platinum Tier (requires 50,000 points earned annually):
- Earn 4 points per $1 spent on eligible travel purchases
- Earn 2 points per $1 spent on all other eligible purchases
- Receive complimentary access to airport VIP lounges with Plaza Premium
- Enjoy waived baggage fees and priority boarding on select airline partners like United and Delta
- Receive a personal travel concierge from Virtuoso for personalized assistance
- Access to exclusive, members-only events and experiences like private winery tours or cooking classes

To maintain your tier status, you'll need to earn the required number of points each year. Your current tier level and progress towards the next tier can be easily tracked within your online account.
