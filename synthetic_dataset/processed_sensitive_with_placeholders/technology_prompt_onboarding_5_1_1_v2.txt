## Azure DevOps Access Information

To access your Azure DevOps account, follow these steps:

1. Open a web browser and navigate to the Azure DevOps organization URL: https://dev.azure.com/globex-devops
2. Click on the "Sign in" button in the top-right corner of the page.
3. Enter your login credentials:
   - **Email**: <USERNAME>
   - **Password**: <PASSWORD>
4. Once you've signed in, you should see the Azure DevOps dashboard, which provides an overview of your team's projects, work items, and other relevant information.

Within your Azure DevOps account, you have been granted the following permissions:

- **Project Access**: You have been added as a member of the "Globex DevOps" project, which gives you access to view, edit, and contribute to all the repositories, work items, and other resources within this project.
- **Repository Access**: You have been granted read and write access to the following repositories:
  - Globex-Infrastructure
  - Globex-WebApp
  - Globex-MobileApp
- **Build and Release Permissions**: You have been granted the ability to view, queue, and manage builds and releases for the projects you have access to.
- **Work Item Management**: You can create, view, edit, and update work items (such as tasks, bugs, and features) for the projects you have access to.

Please note that your access permissions may be different from other team members, depending on your role and responsibilities within the organization.
