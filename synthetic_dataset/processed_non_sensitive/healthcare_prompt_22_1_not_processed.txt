1. Internal Review: If you are dissatisfied with a decision, you can submit a written request for an internal review. Our team will thoroughly investigate the issue and provide you with a written decision within 30 days.

2. External Review: If you are still not satisfied with the internal review decision, you have the right to request an external review by an independent third-party organization. This request must be submitted within 60 days of the internal review decision.

To initiate an appeal, please contact our Member Services team, who will guide you through the necessary steps and documentation requirements. We are committed to ensuring a fair and transparent appeals process.
