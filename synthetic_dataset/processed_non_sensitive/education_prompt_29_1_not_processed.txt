Here is a parent volunteer handbook based on the school's policies and procedures for volunteers:

Parent Volunteer Handbook

Introduction
Thank you for your interest in volunteering at our school! We greatly appreciate the time and effort our parent volunteers contribute to supporting our students and staff. This handbook outlines the school's policies and expectations for parent volunteers to ensure a positive and productive experience for everyone involved.

Volunteer Opportunities
There are many ways parents can get involved at our school, including:
- Classroom assistance (e.g., helping with projects, making copies, organizing supplies)
- Library/media center support (e.g., shelving books, processing new materials)
- School event support (e.g., chaperoning field trips, working the school carnival, setting up for performances)
- PTA/PTO committees (e.g., fundraising, community outreach, hospitality)
- Grounds/facilities maintenance (e.g., gardening, light cleaning, minor repairs)

Volunteer Expectations
We ask that all parent volunteers adhere to the following expectations:

Professionalism: While volunteering, you are representing our school community. We expect volunteers to maintain a professional demeanor, be respectful of all students and staff, and refrain from inappropriate language or behavior.

Confidentiality: In the course of your volunteer work, you may have access to sensitive student or school information. It is crucial that you maintain strict confidentiality and do not share any private details with others.

Reliability: We rely on our volunteers to follow through on their commitments. Please be punctual, communicate any absences or schedule changes in advance, and complete assigned tasks to the best of your ability.

Safety: The safety of our students is our top priority. All volunteers must sign in at the front office, wear a visitor badge, and follow all school safety protocols. Volunteers may not be alone with students without prior approval from the school administration.

Training: Depending on your volunteer role, you may be required to complete specific training (e.g., volunteer orientation, first aid, technology use). Please work with the volunteer coordinator to ensure you are prepared for your responsibilities.

Volunteer Code of Conduct
In addition to the general expectations, we ask that all volunteers abide by the following code of conduct:
- Treat all students, staff, and fellow volunteers with respect, courtesy, and patience.
- Refrain from disciplining students; direct any behavioral concerns to the supervising teacher or staff member.
- Avoid physical contact with students unless it is necessary for the safety or well-being of the child.
- Dress appropriately for the school environment and activity.
- Use appropriate language and refrain from gossip or negative commentary about the school, staff, or students.
- Report any concerns or incidents to the supervising teacher or school administrator immediately.
- Respect the authority of school staff and follow their instructions.

Thank you for your commitment to supporting our school community. We appreciate your time and contributions, and we look forward to working with you!
