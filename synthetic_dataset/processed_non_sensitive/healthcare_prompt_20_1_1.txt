Exclusions and Limitations

While your ABC Health plan provides comprehensive coverage, there are certain services and situations that are excluded or limited. It is important to understand these exclusions and limitations to avoid unexpected out-of-pocket expenses.

Some key exclusions and limitations include:
- Cosmetic procedures: Procedures performed solely to improve appearance, rather than for medical necessity, are generally not covered.
- Experimental or investigational treatments: Novel or unproven therapies that have not been clinically validated are typically excluded.
- Routine foot care: Except for certain conditions, routine foot care, such as toenail trimming, is not covered.
- Infertility treatment: Coverage for infertility diagnosis and treatment is limited to a maximum of 3 cycles per lifetime.
- Custodial care: Long-term, non-skilled care provided to assist with activities of daily living is generally not covered.
- Hearing aids: Coverage for hearing aids is limited to $1,500 per ear every 3 years.
- Chiropractic care: Coverage for chiropractic services is limited to 20 visits per year.

Please refer to your plan documents or contact our Member Services team for a comprehensive list of exclusions and limitations.

Appeals Process

At ABC Health, we are committed to ensuring that our members receive the healthcare services they need. If you disagree with a decision made by ABC Health regarding your coverage or a claim, you have the right to appeal.

The appeals process allows you to request a review of the decision and provide additional information or evidence to support your case. There are two levels of appeal:

Level 1 Appeal
- You have 180 days from the date of the initial decision to request a Level 1 appeal.
- Your request will be reviewed by an ABC Health representative who was not involved in the original decision.
- You will receive a written decision within 30 days of ABC Health receiving your appeal request.

Level 2 Appeal
- If you are not satisfied with the outcome of the Level 1 appeal, you can request a Level 2 appeal.
- Your Level 2 appeal will be reviewed by an independent third-party reviewer.
- You will receive a written decision within 60 days of ABC Health receiving your Level 2 appeal request.

If you are still dissatisfied with the outcome of the Level 2 appeal, you may have the option to pursue external review or legal action. Our Member Services team can provide you with detailed information about the appeals process and assist you in navigating the different levels of appeal.

We encourage you to exhaust the internal appeal process before seeking external review or legal action. ABC Health is committed to working with you to ensure that your healthcare needs are met.
