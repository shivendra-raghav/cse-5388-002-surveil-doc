Course Title: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
Course Number: CS 301
Semester: Fall 2023
Credits: 3
Instructor: Dr. Alex Turing
Instructor Email: aturingPhD@university.edu
Office Hours: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2-4pm

Course Description:
This course provides an introduction to the field of artificial intelligence (AI), exploring the core concepts, methods, and applications of this rapidly evolving discipline. Students will learn about the history and foundational principles of AI, as well as emerging techniques in areas such as machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and intelligent agents. Through a combination of lectures, discussions, and hands-on projects, students will gain a solid understanding of the current state of AI technology and its potential to transform various industries and aspects of our lives.

Course Objectives:
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1. Explain the historical development and key concepts of artificial intelligence.
2. Describe the fundamental algorithms and techniques used in machine learning, natural language processing, and other AI subfields.
3. Analyze the capabilities and limitations of current AI systems and discuss their ethical implications.
4. Apply AI techniques to solve practical problems through the development of basic AI-powered applications.
5. Critically evaluate the societal and technological impacts of artificial intelligence.

Required Textbook:
Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach (4th Edition) by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig
