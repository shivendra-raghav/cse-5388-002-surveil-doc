Introduction
Globaltech Solutions is committed to conducting business with the highest level of integrity and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. Our Code of Conduct ("the Code") outlines the ethical principles and standards that guide the decisions and actions of all Globaltech Solutions employees, officers, and directors.

The Code is designed to help us make informed, ethical choices in our daily work and promote a culture of honesty, accountability, and respect. It covers a range of topics, including conflicts of interest, anti-corruption, protection of company assets, and workplace conduct. All Globaltech Solutions personnel are expected to read, understand, and adhere to the Code.

Ethical Principles
1. Integrity
Globaltech Solutions is committed to the highest standards of honesty, fairness, and ethical behavior. We will be truthful in our dealings with customers, suppliers, competitors, and each other. We will not make false or misleading statements, and we will keep our promises and commitments.

2. Compliance with Laws and Regulations
Globaltech Solutions will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards in the countries where we operate. We will not engage in or support any unlawful activities.

3. Respect for Others
Globaltech Solutions values diversity and inclusion. We will treat all individuals with respect, dignity, and fairness, regardless of their race, color, religion, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, or other protected characteristics. We will not tolerate discrimination, harassment, or bullying in the workplace.

4. Protection of Company Assets
Globaltech Solutions employees are responsible for protecting the company's physical, intellectual, and financial assets from loss, damage, misuse, or theft. We will use company resources only for legitimate business purposes and not for personal gain.

5. Sustainability and Environmental Responsibility
Globaltech Solutions is committed to conducting our business in an environmentally responsible manner. We will strive to minimize our environmental impact, conserve natural resources, and comply with all applicable environmental laws and regulations.

6. Commitment to Quality and Customer Satisfaction
Globaltech Solutions is dedicated to providing high-quality products and services that meet or exceed our customers' expectations. We will communicate openly and honestly with our customers, and we will promptly address any concerns or issues they may have.

Conflicts of Interest
A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests or activities interfere with their ability to make objective and impartial decisions on behalf of Globaltech Solutions. Employees must avoid situations that could create the appearance of a conflict of interest, even if no actual conflict exists.

Examples of potential conflicts of interest include:
- Having a financial interest in a competitor, supplier, or customer of Globaltech Solutions
- Accepting gifts, entertainment, or other benefits from a third party that could influence, or appear to influence, business decisions
- Engaging in outside employment or activities that could compete with Globaltech Solutions' business or distract from an employee's job responsibilities
- Hiring or supervising a family member or close personal friend
- Serving on the board of directors of another company without prior approval from Globaltech Solutions

Globaltech Solutions employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager or the Compliance Officer. The company will then evaluate the situation and determine the appropriate course of action, which may include recusal from decision-making, divestment of the conflicting interest, or other measures to mitigate the conflict.

Reporting Violations
Globaltech Solutions encourages all employees to report any suspected violations of the Code of Conduct, company policies, or applicable laws and regulations. Employees can report concerns through the following channels:

- Directly to their manager or supervisor
- To the Human Resources department
- To the Compliance Officer or a member of the Compliance team
- Through the Globaltech Solutions Ethics Hotline, a confidential and anonymous reporting system

Globaltech Solutions will not tolerate any form of retaliation against employees who make good-faith reports of suspected misconduct. All reports will be investigated promptly and thoroughly, and appropriate disciplinary action will be taken if a violation is found to have occurred.

Employees who are unsure about the appropriate course of action in a given situation are encouraged to seek guidance from their manager, the Compliance Officer, or the Legal department. Globaltech Solutions is committed to fostering an environment where employees feel comfortable raising concerns and asking questions without fear of reprisal.

Consequences
Failure to comply with the Code of Conduct, company policies, or applicable laws and regulations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Disciplinary measures will be determined on a case-by-case basis, taking into account the nature and severity of the violation, the employee's prior record, and any mitigating or aggravating factors.

In addition to disciplinary actions, Globaltech Solutions may also pursue civil or criminal legal action against individuals who engage in unlawful conduct, such as theft, fraud, or bribery. Globaltech Solutions may also be subject to fines, penalties, and other legal consequences for violations of applicable laws and regulations.

Conclusion
Globaltech Solutions' Code of Conduct is a critical component of our commitment to ethical business practices and corporate social responsibility. By upholding the principles and standards set forth in this Code, we can maintain the trust and confidence of our stakeholders and contribute to the long-term success of our company.

All Globaltech Solutions employees are responsible for reading, understanding, and complying with the Code of Conduct. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your manager, the Compliance Officer, or the Human Resources department. Together, we can ensure that Globaltech Solutions remains a leader in our industry and a trusted partner to our customers, suppliers, and the communities in which we operate.
