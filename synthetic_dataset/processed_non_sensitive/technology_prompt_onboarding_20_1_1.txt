Content Management System Setup
In order to begin creating and submitting content for Radiant Media, you'll first need to gain access to our proprietary content management system (CMS). This secure, cloud-based platform is where all of our video assets, metadata, and other essential information are stored and managed.

Accessing the CMS
To access the Radiant Media CMS, you'll need to follow these steps:

1. Visit the Radiant Media content creator portal at https://creators.radiantmedia.com and click the "Sign Up" button.
2. Fill out the registration form with your full name, email address, and a secure password of your choice.
3. Once your account has been approved (typically within 1-2 business days), you'll receive a confirmation email with your login credentials.
4. Use your email and password to log in to the CMS dashboard.

Submitting Content
After gaining access to the CMS, you'll be able to begin uploading and submitting your video content. Here's the process for doing so:

1. Click the "Upload New Content" button on the CMS dashboard.
2. Fill out the required metadata fields, including the video title, description, keywords, and any other relevant information.
3. Drag and drop your video file into the upload area or click "Select File" to choose it from your local storage.
4. Once the upload is complete, review your submission to ensure all information is accurate.
5. Click "Submit for Review" to send your content to our production team for evaluation.

Royalty Payments
As a content creator for Radiant Media, you'll earn royalties based on the performance and viewership of the videos you submit. To ensure you receive proper compensation, please provide the following information when you first sign up for your CMS account:

- Full legal name
- Tax ID number
- Payment method (direct deposit or check)
- Bank account information (if selecting direct deposit)

Our accounting team will use this information to process royalty payments on a quarterly basis. If any of your personal or payment details change, please be sure to update your CMS profile accordingly.

Brand Voice Guidelines
At Radiant Media, we take great pride in our brand identity and the way we present ourselves to our audience. As a content creator, it's crucial that you understand and adhere to our brand voice guidelines to ensure a cohesive and consistent user experience across all of our video content.

Tone and Style
The Radiant Media brand voice should be authoritative yet approachable, knowledgeable yet conversational. We aim to inform and educate our viewers, but also to entertain and inspire them. When crafting your video scripts and on-camera presentations, keep the following guidelines in mind:

- Use clear, concise language that is easy to understand
- Avoid overly technical jargon or industry-specific terminology
- Inject personality and emotion into your delivery to connect with the audience
- Maintain a professional yet friendly demeanor
- Focus on providing value to the viewer rather than simply promoting our company

Messaging and Themes
The core themes and messaging that should be reflected in all Radiant Media video content are:

- Innovation: Showcasing new ideas, technologies, and approaches that are pushing the boundaries of what's possible
- Expertise: Demonstrating our team's deep knowledge and authority within our respective industries
- Inspiration: Motivating our viewers to think creatively, pursue their passions, and strive for personal growth
- Community: Fostering a sense of belonging and connection among our audience members

When developing your video concepts and scripts, be sure to align your ideas with these core brand pillars. This will help ensure that your content not only resonates with our viewers, but also strengthens our overall brand identity.

Visual Identity
In addition to voice and tone, it's also important to maintain consistency in the visual presentation of our brand. This includes:

- Using our approved logo, color palette, and typography in all on-screen graphics
- Adhering to our established filming and editing styles (e.g., camera angles, transitions, music, etc.)
- Ensuring a clean, professional, and visually appealing production quality
- Incorporating our branded assets and imagery (such as title cards, lower thirds, etc.) where appropriate

If you have any questions or need assistance with the visual elements of your video content, please don't hesitate to reach out to our creative director.
