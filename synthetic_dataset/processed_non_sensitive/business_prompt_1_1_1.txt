Onboarding

To ensure a smooth and seamless onboarding process, we have put together this comprehensive documentation to guide you through the necessary steps. Please take the time to review the information carefully and let us know if you have any questions or concerns.

Tutorial for Setup

Setting Up Your Employee Profile
Once you have logged in to the Employee Portal, you will be directed to the "My Profile" section. Here, you can review and update your personal information, such as your contact details, emergency contacts, and any relevant certifications or qualifications.

Completing the Onboarding Forms
In the "Onboarding" section of the portal, you will find a series of forms that need to be completed. These forms include, but are not limited to, the tax forms, employment eligibility verification forms, and the employee handbook acknowledgment. Please ensure that all forms are filled out accurately and submitted as soon as possible.

Accessing Your Benefits
The "Benefits" section of the portal will provide you with detailed information about the various insurance, retirement, and wellness programs available to Globex Solutions employees. You can review the plan options, make any necessary selections, and manage your benefits through this section.

Setting Up Your Workstation
To ensure that you have a comfortable and productive work environment, our IT team will be in touch with you to schedule a time for setting up your workstation. This will include providing you with the necessary hardware, software, and any other equipment or tools required for your role.

Familiarizing Yourself with Company Policies and Procedures
The "Policies" section of the portal contains the Globex Solutions Employee Handbook, which outlines the company's policies, procedures, and expectations. We encourage you to review this handbook thoroughly and familiarize yourself with the information, as it will serve as a valuable resource throughout your employment with Globex Solutions.

Attending Onboarding Meetings and Training Sessions
As part of the onboarding process, you will be required to attend several meetings and training sessions. These sessions are designed to help you better understand Globex Solutions' history, culture, and core values, as well as your specific job responsibilities and the tools and systems you will be using. Your manager will provide you with a schedule of these events, and it is important that you make every effort to attend.

Summary

We are excited to have you join the Globex Solutions team and are confident that you will make valuable contributions to the company. Please remember that we are here to support you throughout your onboarding and beyond. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your manager or the HR team. We wish you all the best in your new role and look forward to working with you.
