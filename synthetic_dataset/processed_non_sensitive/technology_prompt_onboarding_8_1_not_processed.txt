Installation Steps for the Onboardauto Mobile App
The Onboardauto mobile app is a essential tool for managing your e-commerce business on-the-go. Available for both iOS and Android devices, the app provides real-time access to your Onboardauto dashboard, order management system, customer data, and more.

To download and install the Onboardauto mobile app, follow these steps:

1. Open the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) on your mobile device.
2. Search for "Onboardauto" in the search bar.
3. Locate the Onboardauto app in the search results and tap the "Get" or "Install" button to begin the download.
4. Once the download is complete, tap "Open" to launch the app.
5. On the login screen, enter the email address and password associated with your Onboardauto account.
6. If this is your first time logging in, you may be prompted to complete a brief onboarding process. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your mobile app preferences and connect your account.
7. Once you've logged in, you'll be taken to the Onboardauto mobile dashboard, where you can access all the features and functionalities of the platform.

Remember, the Onboardauto mobile app is designed to complement the full web-based platform. While you can perform many essential tasks directly from your mobile device, we recommend also accessing the Onboardauto web application for a more comprehensive management experience.

If you encounter any issues during the installation or login process, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team for assistance.

Employee Details and Permissions
Onboardauto is designed to be a collaborative platform, allowing multiple users to access and manage your e-commerce operations. To get your team set up, you'll need to configure employee details and permissions within the Onboardauto system.

To add a new employee to your Onboardauto account:

1. Log in to the Onboardauto web application and navigate to the "Users" section.
2. Click the "Add User" button to open the user creation form.
3. Fill out the required fields, including the employee's name, email address, and role within your organization.
4. Select the appropriate permissions and access levels for the user. Onboardauto offers a range of pre-defined user roles (e.g., Administrator, Manager, Employee) that you can choose from, or you can create custom permissions.
5. Click "Save" to add the new user to your Onboardauto account.

Once a user has been added, they will receive an email invitation to log in to the Onboardauto platform. They can then download the mobile app and begin accessing the features and functionality relevant to their assigned role.

It's important to note that user permissions can be modified at any time. If an employee's responsibilities change or they need access to additional Onboardauto features, you can easily update their permissions through the "Users" section of the web application.

Additionally, Onboardauto offers advanced user management tools, such as the ability to set up two-factor authentication, export user activity logs, and manage user access across multiple locations or business entities. We recommend reviewing these features to ensure your Onboardauto account is properly secured and aligned with your business needs.

If you have any questions or need assistance with employee onboarding, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
