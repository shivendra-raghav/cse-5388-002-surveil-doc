ABC Corp
Gift and Entertainment Policy

Introduction
ABC Corp ("ABC" or the "Company") is committed to conducting business ethically and in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. This Gift and Entertainment Policy (the "Policy") outlines the guidelines and procedures for giving, receiving, and reporting gifts, entertainment, and other business courtesies in order to prevent conflicts of interest and the appearance of improper influence.

The purpose of this Policy is to ensure that business decisions are made objectively and in the best interests of ABC, without undue influence. Providing or accepting inappropriate gifts or entertainment could be perceived as an attempt to improperly influence business decisions and may violate anti-bribery and corruption laws. All ABC Personnel (defined as employees, officers, and members of the Board of Directors) are responsible for understanding and complying with this Policy.

Acceptable Gifts and Entertainment
Gifts and entertainment can help build goodwill and strengthen business relationships, but they must be reasonable, appropriate, and provided in a transparent manner. ABC Personnel may provide or accept gifts and entertainment that meet the following criteria:

Nominal Value: Gifts and entertainment should be of nominal value, generally not exceeding $75 per recipient per occurrence. Gifts or entertainment of greater value may only be provided with prior written approval from the Chief Compliance Officer.

Appropriate and Customary: Gifts and entertainment must be appropriate to the business relationship, customary for the industry, and consistent with local laws and regulations. They should not be lavish or extravagant.

No Quid Pro Quo: Gifts and entertainment must not be provided with the expectation of receiving anything in return (i.e., a "quid pro quo"). They should not be offered, promised, or provided to improperly influence a business decision.

Transparent and Documented: All gifts and entertainment, regardless of value, must be accurately recorded in ABC's books and records.

Prohibited Gifts and Entertainment
ABC Personnel are prohibited from providing or accepting the following types of gifts and entertainment:

• Cash or cash equivalents (e.g., gift cards, gift certificates, vouchers)
• Gifts or entertainment that are illegal or violate local laws or regulations
• Gifts or entertainment that are sexually oriented or promote the excessive use of alcohol or drugs
• Gifts or entertainment that could be viewed as a bribe, kickback, or other improper payment
• Gifts or entertainment provided in exchange for an improper business advantage

Approval Process
ABC Personnel must obtain prior written approval from their manager and the Compliance Department before providing any gift or entertainment that exceeds $75 in value per recipient. The approval request must include the following information:

• Name and title of the recipient
• Description of the gift or entertainment
• Estimated value of the gift or entertainment
• Business purpose and relationship to ABC
• Confirmation that the gift or entertainment meets the criteria outlined in this Policy

The Compliance Department will review the request to ensure it complies with this Policy and applicable laws and regulations. ABC Personnel must also obtain prior written approval from the Compliance Department before accepting any gift or entertainment that exceeds $75 in value per occurrence.

Recordkeeping
ABC Personnel must accurately record all gifts and entertainment, regardless of value, in the Company's books and records. This includes:

• Description of the gift or entertainment
• Name and title of the giver/recipient
• Estimated value
• Date provided/received
• Business purpose
• Approval documentation, if required

ABC's finance team will conduct periodic reviews of gift and entertainment records to ensure compliance with this Policy.

Violations and Penalties
Violations of this Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. ABC Personnel who violate this Policy may also be subject to civil or criminal penalties under applicable anti-bribery and corruption laws.

ABC encourages all Personnel to report any suspected violations of this Policy to the Compliance Department. ABC prohibits retaliation against any individual who, in good faith, reports a potential violation or cooperates with an investigation.

Seeking Guidance and Reporting Concerns
ABC Personnel who are unsure whether a particular gift or entertainment is acceptable under this Policy should seek guidance from the Compliance Department before providing or accepting it. Similarly, ABC Personnel who become aware of a potential violation of this Policy should report their concerns to the Compliance Department.

Reports of suspected violations can be made through the following channels:

• Compliance Hotline: your unique hotline number
• Email: compliancereports@abccorp.com
• In-person meeting with the Chief Compliance Officer

All reports will be treated confidentially to the extent possible, and ABC prohibits retaliation against any individual who reports a concern in good faith.

Conclusion
ABC is committed to upholding the highest standards of ethical business conduct. This Gift and Entertainment Policy is an important part of ABC's overall compliance program and helps ensure that business decisions are made objectively, without undue influence.

ABC Personnel are expected to familiarize themselves with this Policy and to seek guidance from the Compliance Department when needed. By working together to comply with this Policy, we can protect ABC's reputation and maintain the trust of our customers, partners, and other stakeholders.
