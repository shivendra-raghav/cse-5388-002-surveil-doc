**Networking for Professional Growth - Silicon Valley Tech Meetup**

Join us for an evening of networking and professional development at our monthly Silicon Valley Tech Meetup! This event is perfect for anyone looking to expand their professional network, explore new career opportunities, and learn from experienced industry leaders.

We'll be gathering at the 4th Street Tech Hub, a modern coworking space located in the heart of downtown San Jose. With its open floor plan, natural light, and funky industrial decor, this venue provides the ideal backdrop for making new connections and building meaningful relationships.

The evening will kick off with a 30-minute registration and mixer period, giving attendees the chance to enjoy some light refreshments and begin mingling. We'll then move into a panel discussion featuring three accomplished tech professionals, each offering a unique perspective on navigating the industry, finding a fulfilling career path, and leveraging your network for professional growth.

After the panel, we'll transition into small-group networking activities designed to help you step out of your comfort zone and engage with new people. You'll have the opportunity to participate in speed networking, Q&A sessions, and structured discussions on topics like personal branding, job searching, and entrepreneurship.

Throughout the event, you can also visit our "Experts Corner" to sign up for one-on-one mentoring sessions with seasoned industry veterans who will be on-hand to provide personalized advice and guidance.

Whether you're a recent graduate, a mid-career professional, or an aspiring entrepreneur, this Meetup is the perfect place to make valuable connections, expand your knowledge, and take your career to new heights. We look forward to seeing you there!
