Limitations and Exclusions

While your Acme Health Partners plan provides comprehensive coverage, there are some limitations and exclusions to be aware of. These include:

Limitations:
- Annual limits on certain services (e.g., physical therapy, chiropractic care, skilled nursing facility)
- Pre-authorization requirements for specific procedures or treatments
- Restricted network of healthcare providers for certain services
- Tiered prescription drug formulary with varying copays/coinsurance

Exclusions:
- Cosmetic surgery or treatments, unless medically necessary
- Experimental or investigational procedures
- Routine foot care, unless medically necessary
- Hearing aids (except for children under the age of 18)
- Long-term or custodial nursing home care
- Charges above the reasonable and customary limits

It's important to review these limitations and exclusions carefully to understand what is and is not covered under your plan. If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact our Member Services team.
