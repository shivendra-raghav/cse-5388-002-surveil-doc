Blue Sky Enterprises Travel Policy

Introduction
At Blue Sky Enterprises, we recognize that business travel is essential to the success of our company. Whether visiting clients, attending conferences, or participating in team meetings, travel allows our employees to collaborate, innovate, and develop valuable relationships. However, we also understand the need to exercise financial responsibility and ensure the safety and well-being of our travelers. 

This travel policy outlines the procedures, guidelines, and expectations for all business-related travel undertaken by Blue Sky Enterprises employees. It is the responsibility of each traveler to familiarize themselves with and adhere to the policies set forth in this document. By following these guidelines, we can maximize the value of business travel while minimizing costs and risks.

The policies and procedures detailed in this document apply to all employees, regardless of position or seniority. Managers are expected to lead by example and ensure their direct reports comply with the travel policy. Failure to follow the established guidelines may result in the denial of reimbursement or other disciplinary action.

If you have any questions or need clarification regarding the Blue Sky Enterprises Travel Policy, please contact the Travel Management team at travel@blueskyenterprises.com.

Travel Booking
All business travel must be booked through Blue Sky's approved travel management provider, Corporate Travel Solutions (CTS). CTS offers a full-service solution, including access to a comprehensive database of negotiated rates with airlines, hotels, and car rental agencies. Employees are expected to book the most cost-effective travel arrangements that meet their business needs.

Air Travel
When booking air travel, employees should select the lowest logical airfare available that meets the business need. This includes considering factors such as travel time, number of stops, and overall trip duration. Travelers are permitted to fly in economy class for domestic flights and short-haul international flights (less than 6 hours). Business class is approved for long-haul international flights (6 hours or more).

Employees are encouraged to book flights at least 14 days in advance to take advantage of lower fares. Last-minute bookings should be avoided except in cases of emergency or urgent business needs. 

Blue Sky has negotiated special corporate rates with the following airlines: American Airlines, Delta Air Lines, and United Airlines. Whenever possible, travelers should book flights with one of these preferred carriers to maximize cost savings and earn loyalty rewards.

Hotel Accommodations
For hotel reservations, employees should book rooms at properties that offer negotiated corporate rates through CTS. These rates are pre-approved and ensure Blue Sky is receiving the best available pricing. If a preferred hotel is not available, travelers may book alternative accommodations, but should still seek the most cost-effective option.

Employees are expected to book standard room types, avoiding suites or other upgraded accommodations unless there is a valid business justification. The maximum nightly rate for hotel stays is $250 for domestic travel and $350 for international travel. Exceptions may be granted by the Travel Management team for high-cost destinations or special circumstances.

Ground Transportation
Blue Sky has established partnerships with several car rental agencies to provide discounted rates for business travel. Employees should book rentals through CTS and select the most economical vehicle size that meets their needs. Luxury or premium vehicles are not permitted except with prior approval from the Travel Management team.

For local transportation, such as taxi, rideshare, or public transit, employees should select the most cost-effective and efficient option. Rideshare services like Uber and Lyft are generally preferred over traditional taxis due to their competitive pricing and convenience. Employees are encouraged to use public transportation whenever feasible, as it is often the most economical choice.

Transportation to and from the airport should be booked through CTS or using a personal vehicle, if more cost-effective. Employees may claim mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use at the current IRS rate.

Accommodation
Blue Sky Enterprises is committed to ensuring the comfort and safety of our employees while traveling on business. The following guidelines apply to hotel accommodations:

Room Preferences
Employees should book standard room types, avoiding suites or other upgraded accommodations unless there is a valid business justification. The maximum nightly rate for hotel stays is $250 for domestic travel and $350 for international travel. Exceptions may be granted by the Travel Management team for high-cost destinations or special circumstances.

When booking hotels, travelers should consider the following preferences:
- Rooms with king-size beds or two double/queen beds
- Non-smoking rooms
- Rooms with desks or work areas
- Rooms with complimentary Wi-Fi access
- Rooms with complimentary breakfast, if available

Hotel Loyalty Programs
Employees are encouraged to enroll in hotel loyalty programs, such as Marriott Bonvoy or Hilton Honors, to earn rewards and benefits during their stays. However, loyalty program status should not be the primary factor when selecting accommodations. Travelers must still prioritize cost-effective options that meet the company's hotel rate guidelines.

Extended Stays
For business trips longer than 5 nights, employees may consider booking extended-stay hotels or serviced apartments. These accommodations often provide more living space, cooking facilities, and other amenities that can improve comfort and reduce meal expenses during prolonged trips.

Expense Reporting
Employees are responsible for accurately reporting all business-related travel expenses incurred during the course of their duties. The following guidelines apply to the expense reporting process:

Expense Reports
All travel expenses must be submitted using Blue Sky's approved expense reporting system, Concur. Employees should log their expenses, attach supporting documentation (e.g., receipts, invoices), and submit the report within 14 days of completing their trip.

Acceptable Expenses
Blue Sky will reimburse employees for the following business-related travel expenses:
- Airfare and other transportation costs (e.g., train, bus, rideshare, taxi)
- Hotel accommodations
- Meals and incidentals (subject to per diem limits)
- Parking and tolls
- Baggage fees
- Business supplies or equipment purchased during the trip
- Laundry and dry cleaning for trips longer than 5 days
- Tips and gratuities (up to 20% of the total bill)

Unacceptable Expenses
Blue Sky will not reimburse employees for the following personal or non-essential expenses:
- Upgrades to first or business class (unless pre-approved)
- In-flight entertainment or Wi-Fi charges
- Minibar purchases or alcoholic beverages (except with meals)
- Spa treatments, gym fees, or recreational activities
- Personal entertainment (e.g., movies, concerts, sporting events)
- Pet boarding or pet-related expenses
- Lost or stolen items
- Traffic violations or parking tickets

Per Diem Allowances
Blue Sky provides a daily per diem allowance to cover meals and incidental expenses while traveling. The per diem rates are based on the destination's cost of living and are as follows:
- Domestic travel: $55 per day
- International travel: $75 per day

Employees are not required to submit receipts for per diem expenses but must still report the total amount spent each day. Any meals provided by the hotel, airline, or event host should be deducted from the per diem allowance.

Expense Reimbursement
Approved expense reports will be processed for reimbursement within 10 business days. Employees can choose to receive their reimbursement via direct deposit or check. All reimbursements are subject to the guidelines and limits outlined in this travel policy.
