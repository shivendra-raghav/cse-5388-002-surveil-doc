Anti-Harassment Policy
Acme Corporation

Introduction
Acme Corporation is committed to providing a work environment that is free from all forms of harassment and discrimination. We believe that all employees have the right to be treated with respect and dignity, and we will not tolerate any behavior that undermines this fundamental right. This Anti-Harassment Policy outlines the company's position on harassment, the procedures for reporting incidents, and the actions that will be taken in response to violations of this policy.

Definition of Harassment
Harassment is defined as any unwelcome conduct that is based on an individual's race, color, religion, national origin, age, disability, sex (including pregnancy), gender identity, sexual orientation, or any other legally protected characteristic. Harassment can take many forms, including but not limited to:

• Verbal harassment: Epithets, derogatory comments, slurs, or unwanted sexual advances, invitations, or comments.
• Physical harassment: Unwanted physical contact, assault, impeding or blocking movements, or any intimidating interference with normal work or movement.
• Visual harassment: Derogatory posters, cartoons, drawings, or gestures.
• Sexual harassment: Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature when:

1. Submission to such conduct is made either explicitly or implicitly a term or condition of an individual's employment.
2. Submission to or rejection of such conduct by an individual is used as the basis for employment decisions affecting such individual.
3. Such conduct has the purpose or effect of unreasonably interfering with an individual's work performance or creating an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment.

Harassment can occur in a variety of circumstances, including but not limited to the workplace, work-related settings, and any other location where the harassing conduct has the purpose or effect of unreasonably interfering with an individual's work performance or creating an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment.

Prohibited Conduct
Acme Corporation strictly prohibits all forms of harassment and discrimination in the workplace. Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

• Verbal harassment, such as derogatory comments, slurs, or unwanted sexual advances, invitations, or comments.
• Physical harassment, such as unwanted physical contact, assault, impeding or blocking movements, or any intimidating interference with normal work or movement.
• Visual harassment, such as derogatory posters, cartoons, drawings, or gestures.
• Sexual harassment, such as unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature.
• Retaliation against an individual for filing a complaint of harassment or discrimination, or for participating in an investigation of such a complaint.
• Failure to investigate and address reported incidents of harassment or discrimination.

This list is not exhaustive, and any behavior that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment is strictly prohibited.

Reporting Procedure
Acme Corporation encourages all employees who believe they have been subjected to or witnessed harassment or discrimination to promptly report the incident. Employees have several options for reporting:

1. Reporting to a supervisor or manager: Employees should feel comfortable reporting any incidents of harassment or discrimination to their immediate supervisor or manager. Supervisors and managers are required to immediately report any incidents of harassment or discrimination to the Human Resources Department.

2. Reporting to the Human Resources Department: Employees can also report incidents directly to the Human Resources Department. The HR Department can be contacted by phone, email, or in-person. Employees can also submit a written complaint to the HR Department.

3. Anonymous reporting: Acme Corporation has an anonymous reporting hotline that employees can use to report incidents of harassment or discrimination. The hotline is staffed by an independent third-party and is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Employees who report incidents of harassment or discrimination in good faith will be protected from retaliation. Acme Corporation strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against an employee for filing a complaint or participating in an investigation.

Investigation
All reports of harassment or discrimination will be promptly and thoroughly investigated. The investigation will be conducted by the Human Resources Department or a designated third-party investigator. The investigation will include:

1. Interviewing the complainant, the accused, and any witnesses.
2. Reviewing any relevant documentation or evidence.
3. Determining whether the conduct violates this policy.
4. Recommending appropriate corrective action, if necessary.

The investigation will be conducted in a manner that protects the privacy and confidentiality of the individuals involved, to the extent possible. Acme Corporation will make every effort to keep the investigation and its results confidential.

If the investigation determines that harassment or discrimination has occurred, Acme Corporation will take prompt and appropriate corrective action. Corrective action may include, but is not limited to:

• Disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
• Training or counseling for the accused individual.
• Changes to the work environment or job duties to address the harassment or discrimination.
• Monitoring the accused individual to ensure that the harassment or discrimination does not continue.

Acme Corporation will also take steps to prevent retaliation against the complainant or any individual who participated in the investigation.

Conclusion
Acme Corporation is committed to creating and maintaining a work environment that is free from harassment and discrimination. We will not tolerate any behavior that undermines the dignity and respect of our employees. All employees are expected to abide by this Anti-Harassment Policy and to report any incidents of harassment or discrimination. Acme Corporation will investigate all reports promptly and take appropriate corrective action.

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact the Human Resources Department.
