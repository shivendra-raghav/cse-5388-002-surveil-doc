Introduction
This Safety Plan outlines the procedures and protocols to be followed in the event of an emergency at Oakwood High School. The safety of our students, staff, and visitors is our top priority, and this plan is designed to ensure that our school is prepared to respond effectively to a variety of emergency situations.

Emergency Procedures
In the event of an emergency, the school administration will immediately assess the situation and determine the appropriate course of action. The following emergency procedures will be followed, depending on the nature of the crisis:

1. Evacuation Procedures:
   a. In the event of a fire, explosion, or other situation that requires the immediate evacuation of the building, the fire alarm will be activated.
   b. Students and staff will exit the building through the nearest safe exit and proceed to the designated assembly area, which is the soccer field behind the school.
   c. Teachers will take attendance and report any missing students to the principal or their designee.
   d. The principal or their designee will notify emergency responders and provide them with any relevant information.
   e. Students and staff will remain at the assembly area until the all-clear signal is given or until further instructions are provided.

2. Lockdown Procedures:
   a. In the event of an intruder or other situation that requires the school to be locked down, the principal or their designee will initiate the lockdown procedure.
   b. All students and staff will immediately go to the nearest secure location and lock the doors.
   c. Lights will be turned off, and everyone will remain quiet and out of sight.
   d. Teachers will take attendance and report any missing or additional students to the principal or their designee.
   e. The principal or their designee will notify the appropriate emergency responders and provide them with any relevant information.
   f. Students and staff will remain in the secure location until the all-clear signal is given or until further instructions are provided.

3. Shelter-in-Place Procedures:
   a. In the event of a severe weather event, such as a tornado or severe thunderstorm, the principal or their designee will initiate the shelter-in-place procedure.
   b. Students and staff will move to the designated shelter areas, which are the interior hallways and classrooms without windows.
   c. Teachers will take attendance and report any missing or additional students to the principal or their designee.
   d. Students and staff will remain in the shelter areas until the all-clear signal is given or until further instructions are provided.

Communication Plan
In the event of an emergency, the school administration will use the following communication methods to inform students, staff, and parents/guardians:
- School-wide intercom system
- Email and text message alerts
- Social media posts
- Local media outlets (radio, television, and newspapers)

The principal or their designee will be responsible for coordinating all communication efforts and providing updates as necessary.

Conclusion
This Safety Plan outlines the procedures and protocols to be followed in the event of an emergency at Oakwood High School. By being prepared and practicing these procedures regularly, we can ensure the safety of our students, staff, and visitors. If you have any questions or concerns about this plan, please contact the school administration.
