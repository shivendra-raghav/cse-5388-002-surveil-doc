Activity Schedule
Day 1 (June 1): Arrival and Orientation
- Arrive at Charles de Gaulle Airport and transfer to your hotel
- Check-in and take some time to settle in
- Explore the neighborhood around your hotel and get a feel for the local atmosphere
- Enjoy a welcome dinner at a traditional French bistro

Day 2 (June 2): The Iconic Eiffel Tower
- Start your day with a leisurely breakfast at the hotel
- Head to the Eiffel Tower and take in the breathtaking views of the city
- Explore the nearby Champ de Mars and its charming gardens
- Visit the Musée du Quai Branly, featuring impressive collections of indigenous art and artifacts
- Enjoy a classic French lunch at a cozy café

Day 3 (June 3): The Louvre and Montmartre
- Begin your day at the iconic Louvre Museum, where you'll admire world-famous masterpieces, such as the Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo
- Take a stroll through the enchanting neighborhood of Montmartre, known for its charming streets, artist studios, and the iconic Sacré-Cœur Basilica
- Explore the quaint shops and cafes in the area, and perhaps catch a performance by street artists
- Indulge in a delectable dinner at a local restaurant in Montmartre

Day 4 (June 4): The Seine River and Notre-Dame Cathedral
- Start your day with a relaxing cruise along the Seine River, taking in the stunning views of the city's landmarks
- Visit the iconic Notre-Dame Cathedral, and marvel at its impressive Gothic architecture
- Spend the afternoon exploring the nearby Latin Quarter, known for its vibrant student life, bookstores, and trendy boutiques
- Enjoy a picnic lunch in the Luxembourg Gardens

Day 5 (June 5): The Arc de Triomphe and Champs-Élysées
- Ascend to the top of the Arc de Triomphe for panoramic views of the city
- Stroll down the famous Champs-Élysées, lined with high-end shops, restaurants, and cafes
- Visit the Grand Palais and Petit Palais, two stunning examples of French architecture
- Indulge in a delightful afternoon tea at a renowned Parisian patisserie

Day 6 (June 6): Versailles Excursion
- Embark on a day trip to the magnificent Palace of Versailles, a UNESCO World Heritage site
- Explore the opulent halls, lavish gardens, and the iconic Hall of Mirrors
- Wander through the beautiful Marie Antoinette's Estate and the Grand Trianon
- Return to Paris in the late afternoon and enjoy a farewell dinner at a local brasserie

Day 7 (June 7): Free Day
- Enjoy a leisurely morning and explore any remaining sites or neighborhoods at your own pace
- Visit the Musée d'Orsay, housed in a former train station, and admire its impressive collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist art
- Indulge in some last-minute shopping or simply relax in one of the city's charming parks

Day 8 (June 8): Departure
- Check out of your hotel and transfer to Charles de Gaulle Airport for your return flight home
