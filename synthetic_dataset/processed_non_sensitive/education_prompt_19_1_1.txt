Dear Parents/Guardians,

We are excited to announce an upcoming educational field trip for the 5th-grade students at Willow Grove Academy. On Friday, June 9th, 2023, the students will be visiting the Natural History Museum in Metropolis City.

This field trip is an excellent opportunity for our students to enhance their learning and broaden their understanding of the natural world. The museum's extensive collection of artifacts, fossils, and interactive exhibits will provide a unique and engaging educational experience.

Purpose and Objectives of the Field Trip:
The purpose of this field trip is to complement the students' classroom learning in science, specifically in the areas of paleontology, geology, and zoology. By exploring the museum's exhibits, students will have the chance to:
- Observe and learn about the evolution of life on Earth through the study of fossils and prehistoric specimens.
- Discover the diversity of the natural world by examining the museum's collection of plant and animal specimens.
- Gain a deeper understanding of the geological processes that have shaped our planet, such as plate tectonics and the formation of rocks and minerals.
- Engage in hands-on learning activities and interactive displays to reinforce their understanding of scientific concepts.

Trip Details:
Date: Friday, June 9th, 2023
Departure Time: 8:30 a.m. from Willow Grove Academy
Arrival Time at the Museum: 10:00 a.m.
Departure Time from the Museum: 2:00 p.m.
Arrival Time back at Willow Grove Academy: 3:30 p.m.

Transportation:
Students will be transported to and from the Natural History Museum by school buses. Our experienced and licensed school bus drivers will ensure the safe transport of our students.

Supervision and Safety:
The field trip will be supervised by the 5th-grade teachers, as well as several parent chaperones. The chaperones will be responsible for monitoring the students' behavior and ensuring their safety throughout the trip. All necessary safety precautions will be taken, and students will be instructed on appropriate behavior and etiquette while visiting the museum.

Lunch and Snacks:
Students will be provided with a boxed lunch, which will include a sandwich, a fruit cup, a granola bar, and a bottle of water. Please inform us of any dietary restrictions or allergies your child may have.

Cost and Payment:
The cost of the field trip, which includes transportation, admission to the museum, and the boxed lunch, is $18 per student. Please submit the payment in cash or by check (made payable to Willow Grove Academy) by Monday, June 5th, 2023.

Permission Slip and Emergency Contact Information:
To allow your child to participate in this field trip, please complete the attached permission slip and return it to your child's teacher by Monday, June 5th, 2023. It is essential that we have up-to-date emergency contact information for each student, so please ensure that the information provided on the permission slip is accurate and current.

We are confident that this field trip will be an enriching and enjoyable experience for our students. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the school office at (555) 123-4567.

Thank you for your support and cooperation.
