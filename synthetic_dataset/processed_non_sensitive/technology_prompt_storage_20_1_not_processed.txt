Once your account has been created, you'll be able to access the Filebase web interface, where you can manage your files, buckets, and other storage-related tasks. The web interface provides a user-friendly dashboard that allows you to easily navigate and interact with your Filebase account.

The CLI tool, called the Filebase CLI, can be downloaded from the Filebase website and installed on your local machine. To use the Filebase CLI, you'll need to authenticate your account by providing your Filebase API key and secret. In addition to the CLI, Filebase also provides a comprehensive API that allows you to integrate Filebase functionality directly into your applications and workflows. One of the key features of the Filebase platform is its support for multi-cloud storage. Unlike traditional cloud storage providers that lock you into a single cloud platform, Filebase allows you to store your data across multiple cloud providers, including Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage, and Microsoft Azure Blob Storage.

This multi-cloud approach offers several benefits:

1. Increased Reliability: By distributing your data across multiple cloud providers, you can reduce the risk of data loss or downtime due to a single provider's outage or failure.

2. Improved Resilience: Filebase's decentralized architecture ensures that your data is replicated across multiple cloud providers, making it more resistant to natural disasters, cyber attacks, or other disruptions that may affect a single cloud provider.

3. Enhanced Data Sovereignty: With Filebase's multi-cloud support, you can choose to store your data in specific regions or jurisdictions that align with your organization's data sovereignty requirements.

To take advantage of Filebase's multi-cloud support, you can simply create buckets in the Filebase web interface or using the Filebase CLI or API, and specify the cloud provider(s) you want to use for that bucket. Filebase will then automatically manage the distribution and replication of your data across the selected cloud providers, ensuring that your files are always available and secure.

When accessing your files, the Filebase platform will transparently handle the underlying cloud storage details, allowing you to interact with your data using the same S3-compatible API and tools that you're already familiar with. This makes it easy to migrate your existing applications and workflows to Filebase without having to make significant changes to your codebase.
