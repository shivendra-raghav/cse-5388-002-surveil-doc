Onboarding Guide
Welcome to Acme Health Systems! We are excited to have you as a new patient and look forward to providing you with high-quality, personalized care. To ensure a smooth onboarding process, please review the following information.

Getting Started
To begin, you will need to complete our new patient registration form, either online or in person at our front desk. This form will collect basic information about you, including your name, date of birth, contact details, and insurance information. Please have this information readily available.

Once your registration is complete, you will be assigned a unique patient ID number that will be used to track your medical records and future visits. Please reference this number in all communications with our office.

Scheduling Your First Appointment
After registering, you can schedule your first appointment with one of our primary care providers. Our team will work with you to find a convenient date and time that fits your schedule. During this initial visit, your provider will conduct a comprehensive health assessment, review your medical history, and develop a personalized care plan.

If you require specialty care, such as with a cardiologist or orthopedist, your primary care provider will coordinate a referral and help you schedule the appropriate appointment.

Accessing Your Medical Records
You have the right to access your complete medical records at any time. To request a copy, please submit a signed authorization form, available at our front desk or website. We will process your request within 30 days and provide the records securely.

Coverage Details
Our healthcare institute accepts a wide range of insurance plans, including Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, and Blue Cross Blue Shield. We are committed to ensuring that you receive the maximum coverage allowed under your plan.

Covered Services
Your plan provides coverage for the following services:
- Annual wellness exams and preventive care
- Sick visits and treatment for acute illnesses
- Diagnostic testing, such as blood work and imaging
- Specialist consultations and treatments
- Inpatient hospital care
- Rehabilitation services, including physical therapy
- Prescription drug coverage

Deductibles and Cost-Sharing
Your plan has an annual deductible of $1,500, which means you will be responsible for paying the first $1,500 of covered medical expenses before your insurance begins to cover a portion of the costs. Once you have met your deductible, you will be required to pay a 20% coinsurance for in-network services until you reach your annual out-of-pocket maximum of $5,000.

Please note that out-of-network services may be subject to higher deductibles and coinsurance rates. We encourage you to review your plan details carefully and contact your insurance provider if you have any questions about your coverage.

Appeals Process
We understand that there may be times when you disagree with a coverage decision made by your insurance provider. In these cases, you have the right to file an appeal.

Step 1: Internal Review
If you believe your insurance provider has incorrectly denied a claim or service, you should first contact them directly to request an internal review. This process typically involves submitting additional documentation or information to support your case.

Step 2: External Review
If you are not satisfied with the outcome of the internal review, you may be able to request an external review by an independent, third-party organization. This process is overseen by your state's insurance department and can provide an impartial assessment of your case.

Step 3: Expedited Review
In certain urgent situations, such as a life-threatening condition or the need for immediate treatment, you may be eligible for an expedited review process. This can help ensure that you receive the necessary care without delay.

We are committed to supporting you throughout the appeals process and will work closely with your insurance provider to advocate on your behalf. If you need assistance, please contact our patient advocate team.
