Club Charter: The Robotics and Automation Club

Article I: Purpose and Mission
The Robotics and Automation Club at Maple Valley High School is dedicated to fostering a passion for engineering, technology, and innovation among students. Our primary mission is to provide a platform for students to explore and engage with the rapidly evolving field of robotics and automation, with a focus on hands-on learning, collaborative projects, and the application of cutting-edge technologies.

The club aims to:
1. Encourage and facilitate the exploration of robotics, programming, and automation technologies.
2. Provide opportunities for students to design, build, and program their own robotic systems and devices.
3. Organize workshops, seminars, and guest speaker events to expose members to the latest advancements and career opportunities in the field of robotics and automation.
4. Participate in local, regional, and national robotics competitions and hackathons, allowing members to showcase their skills and compete against their peers.
5. Promote interdisciplinary collaboration and the integration of various STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) disciplines.
6. Organize community outreach initiatives to inspire and educate younger students about the wonders of robotics and technology.

Article II: Membership and Eligibility
Membership in the Robotics and Automation Club is open to all students enrolled at Maple Valley High School, regardless of their academic major or prior experience in robotics. The club welcomes members with diverse backgrounds and skill sets, encouraging collaboration and the exchange of ideas.

To become a member, students must:
1. Complete and submit the club membership application form.
2. Attend at least two club meetings or events within the first month of joining.
3. Demonstrate a genuine interest in robotics, automation, or related STEM fields.

Membership in the club is renewable on an annual basis, and members are expected to actively participate in club activities and contribute to the overall success of the organization.

Article III: Club Meetings and Activities
The Robotics and Automation Club will hold regular meetings on a bi-weekly basis, typically on Thursdays at 4:00 PM. These meetings will serve as a platform for members to share ideas, collaborate on projects, and discuss the latest advancements in the field of robotics and automation.

In addition to the regular meetings, the club will organize various activities and events throughout the academic year, including:
1. Hands-on workshops and tutorials on topics such as programming, circuit design, and mechanical engineering.
2. Field trips to local robotics companies, research laboratories, or technology hubs to expose members to real-world applications of robotics and automation.
3. Participation in regional and national robotics competitions, where members can showcase their skills and learn from their peers.
4. Collaborative projects, where members can form teams and work together to design, build, and program their own robotic systems.
5. Guest speaker events featuring industry professionals, academic researchers, or successful alumni, who will share their experiences and insights with club members.
6. Community outreach initiatives, such as robotics demonstrations or STEM education workshops for local schools or community centers.

Article IV: Club Leadership and Governance
The Robotics and Automation Club will be led by an executive board, consisting of the following positions:
1. President: Responsible for overseeing the overall operations of the club, setting the strategic direction, and presiding over club meetings.
2. Vice President: Assists the President in their duties and assumes the President's responsibilities in their absence.
3. Treasurer: Responsible for managing the club's finances, including the collection of membership fees, the allocation of funds for club activities, and the maintenance of financial records.
4. Secretary: Responsible for recording meeting minutes, maintaining the club's records, and communicating with club members.
5. Events Coordinator: Responsible for planning and organizing the club's various activities, workshops, and events.
6. Outreach Coordinator: Responsible for developing and implementing the club's community outreach initiatives and partnerships.

The executive board members will be elected annually by the club membership, and they will serve for a term of one academic year. Elections will be held in September of each year, and any active club member in good standing is eligible to run for a position on the executive board.

Article V: Club Funds and Financial Management
The Robotics and Automation Club will generate funds through the collection of membership fees, as well as through fundraising activities and sponsorships. The club's financial management will be overseen by the Treasurer, who will be responsible for the following:

1. Membership Fees: All club members will be required to pay an annual membership fee of $20. These fees will be used to cover the costs of club operations, including the purchase of materials and equipment, the funding of club activities and events, and the participation in robotics competitions.

2. Fundraising Activities: The club may organize various fundraising initiatives, such as bake sales, car washes, or crowdfunding campaigns, to supplement its financial resources. All funds raised through these activities will be deposited into the club's account and used to support the club's operations and initiatives.

3. Sponsorships: The club will seek sponsorships from local businesses, industry partners, and alumni to support its activities and initiatives. These sponsorships may take the form of monetary donations, in-kind contributions, or the provision of resources and mentorship.

4. Financial Reporting: The Treasurer will be responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, including income and expenditure statements, and will provide regular updates on the club's financial status to the executive board and the club membership.

5. Expenditures: All club expenditures must be approved by the executive board and will be made in accordance with the club's budget and the school's financial policies. The Treasurer will ensure that all funds are used in a responsible and transparent manner to support the club's mission and objectives.

Article VI: Amendments and Ratification
This club charter may be amended by a two-thirds majority vote of the club membership. Proposed amendments must be submitted in writing to the executive board at least 2 weeks prior to a club meeting, where they will be discussed and voted upon.

This club charter has been ratified by the founding members of the Robotics and Automation Club on May 15, 2023, and will take effect immediately.
