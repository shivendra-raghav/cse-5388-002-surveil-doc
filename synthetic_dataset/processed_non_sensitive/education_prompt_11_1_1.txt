School District Budget Proposal for the 2023-2024 Fiscal Year

Introduction
The ABC Unified School District is dedicated to providing a high-quality education for all students within our community. In order to fulfill this mission, we must ensure adequate funding for critical educational resources, staff, and programming. This budget proposal outlines the financial needs and priorities of our district for the upcoming 2023-2024 fiscal year.

Revenue Sources and Allocation Procedures
The ABC Unified School District receives funding from a variety of sources, including federal, state, and local government allocations, as well as private donations and grants. Federal funding is largely determined by student enrollment and demographics, and is used to support programs for students with special needs, English language learners, and students from low-income families. State funding is based on a formula that takes into account factors such as property values, student population, and educational outcomes. Local funding comes primarily from property taxes levied by the district. 

All revenue sources are carefully monitored and allocated through an established budgeting process. This process involves collaboration between district administration, the school board, and community stakeholders. Budget proposals are developed by the district's finance department, reviewed by the superintendent, and approved by the school board. Funds are then distributed to individual schools and departments based on enrollment, program needs, and strategic priorities.

Expenditure Priorities
The ABC Unified School District has identified the following key expenditure priorities for the 2023-2024 fiscal year:

1. Staffing and Compensation: Ensuring competitive salaries and benefits in order to attract and retain high-quality teachers and support staff is a top priority. We aim to provide a 3% cost-of-living adjustment for all employees, as well as step increases for eligible staff.

2. Instructional Resources: Providing students with up-to-date textbooks, technology, and other learning materials is essential for supporting academic achievement. We plan to allocate funds for curriculum updates, software licenses, and classroom supplies.

3. Facilities Maintenance and Improvement: Maintaining safe, functional, and modern school buildings is critical. This budget includes funds for routine maintenance, as well as selected renovation and expansion projects.

4. Student Support Services: Allocating resources for counseling, mental health services, special education, and English language instruction is vital for meeting the diverse needs of our student population.

5. Professional Development: Investing in high-quality training and continued education for teachers and administrators helps ensure instructional excellence and implementation of best practices.

Conclusion
The ABC Unified School District is committed to being responsible stewards of public funds in order to provide the best possible educational experience for our students. This budget proposal reflects our strategic priorities and financial needs for the 2023-2024 school year. We welcome feedback and collaboration from our community as we work to finalize and implement this plan.
