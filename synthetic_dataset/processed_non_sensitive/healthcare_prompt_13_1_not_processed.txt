Prescription Benefits:
Your healthcare plan includes a robust prescription drug benefit. You have access to a wide range of medications, both generic and brand-name, to address your healthcare needs. Out-of-Network Coverage:
While our in-network providers offer the most comprehensive and cost-effective care, we understand that there may be instances where you need to seek treatment from an out-of-network healthcare professional. In such cases, your plan provides coverage, albeit with some limitations.

For out-of-network services, you will be responsible for a higher deductible and coinsurance rate. Additionally, the provider may charge you the difference between the billed amount and the amount we deem reasonable and customary. It is important to keep in mind that any out-of-network care may require prior authorization to ensure coverage.

If you anticipate the need for out-of-network care, we recommend contacting our customer service team to discuss your options and understand the potential financial implications. This will help you make an informed decision and minimize any unexpected costs.

Appeals Process:
We understand that there may be instances where you disagree with a decision made regarding your healthcare coverage. In such cases, you have the right to appeal the decision through our established appeals process.

The first step in the appeals process is to request a reconsideration of the decision. This involves submitting additional medical documentation or evidence that supports your case. Our team of medical experts will review the information and provide a decision within a specified timeframe.

If you are still dissatisfied with the outcome of the reconsideration, you have the option to file a formal appeal. This will involve a more comprehensive review of your case by an independent panel of healthcare professionals. During this process, you may also have the opportunity to provide additional information or testimony to support your position.

It is important to note that there are strict deadlines for submitting appeals. We encourage you to familiarize yourself with the appeals process and to reach out to our customer service team if you have any questions or need assistance throughout the process.
