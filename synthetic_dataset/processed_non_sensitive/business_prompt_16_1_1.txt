REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Procurement of Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) System

Introduction
Globex Solutions is a leading manufacturer of high-quality office equipment with a growing international presence. We are seeking to implement a new Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system to streamline our business operations and support our continued growth. The selected ERP system should provide integrated solutions for financial management, supply chain optimization, human resource management, and other critical business functions.

This Request for Proposal (RFP) outlines our requirements and invites qualified vendors to submit their proposals for the implementation of an ERP system that meets our needs. The successful vendor will be responsible for the design, deployment, and ongoing support of the new ERP system.

Scope of Work
The scope of work for the ERP system implementation project includes the following key components:

1. System Selection and Configuration:
   a. Conduct a thorough assessment of Globex's current business processes and IT infrastructure to identify the specific requirements for the new ERP system.
   b. Evaluate and recommend the most suitable ERP solution that aligns with our business objectives and technical requirements.
   c. Configure the selected ERP system to meet Globex's unique needs, including customization of modules, integration with existing systems, and data migration from legacy systems.

2. Implementation and Deployment:
   a. Develop a detailed project plan and timeline for the implementation of the new ERP system.
   b. Provide a comprehensive training program for Globex's employees to ensure smooth adoption and effective utilization of the ERP system.
   c. Ensure a seamless transition from the current system to the new ERP system, minimizing business disruptions.
   d. Perform rigorous testing and quality assurance measures to validate the system's functionality and data integrity.

3. Integration and Connectivity:
   a. Integrate the ERP system with Globex's existing IT infrastructure, including financial systems, customer relationship management (CRM) software, and supply chain management tools.
   b. Ensure secure and reliable data exchange between the ERP system and other critical business applications.
   c. Implement robust data management and reporting capabilities to support informed decision-making.

4. Ongoing Support and Maintenance:
   a. Provide comprehensive user support and technical assistance throughout the project implementation and beyond.
   b. Develop a comprehensive maintenance plan to ensure the long-term stability and continuous improvement of the ERP system.
   c. Deliver regular software updates, security patches, and system enhancements to keep the ERP system aligned with Globex's evolving business needs.

Timeline
Globex Solutions expects the ERP system implementation project to be completed within 12 months from the contract award date. The proposed timeline is as follows:

Phase 1 (3 months): System Selection and Configuration

- Evaluate and select the most suitable ERP solution

Phase 2 (6 months): Implementation and Deployment
- Develop a detailed project plan and timeline
- Provide comprehensive employee training

Phase 3 (3 months): Go-live and Transition Support
- Facilitate the transition from the current system to the new ERP system
- Provide ongoing user support and technical assistance

Vendors should provide a detailed project timeline in their proposals, outlining the key milestones, deliverables, and resource requirements for each phase of the implementation.

Evaluation Criteria
Globex Solutions will evaluate the submitted proposals based on the following criteria:

1. Technical Capabilities (35%):
   - Alignment of the proposed ERP solution with Globex's business requirements
   - Functionality, scalability, and integration capabilities of the ERP system
   - Vendor's technical expertise and experience in implementing similar ERP projects

2. Implementation Approach (30%):
   - Comprehensive project plan and timeline
   - Effectiveness of the deployment methodology and change management strategy
   - Quality of the proposed training and support services

3. Vendor Qualifications (20%):
   - Vendor's track record and industry reputation
   - Relevant experience in the manufacturing and distribution sectors
   - Financial stability and resource availability

4. Total Cost of Ownership (15%):
   - Competitive pricing for the ERP system and implementation services
   - Ongoing maintenance and support costs
   - Flexibility and scalability of the pricing model
