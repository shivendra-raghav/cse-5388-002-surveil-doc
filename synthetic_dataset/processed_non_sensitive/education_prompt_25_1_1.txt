Incident Description:
On March 15, 2023, at approximately 10:20 AM, an incident occurred in the Science Laboratory at Oak Grove High School. The incident involved a student, Michael Johnson, and a teacher, Mrs. Emily Wilson.

According to the witness, Sarah Thompson, the incident began when Michael Johnson, a student in Grade 10, became disruptive during a lesson in Mrs. Wilson's class. Michael Johnson was reportedly making loud noises, distracting other students, and refusing to follow instructions.

Mrs. Wilson attempted to address the situation by asking Michael Johnson to stop the disruptive behavior and follow the classroom rules. However, Michael Johnson continued to be disruptive and refused to comply with Mrs. Wilson's instructions.

The incident escalated, and Mrs. Wilson had to call for assistance from the school administration. The principal and a security officer responded to the situation, and Michael Johnson was escorted out of the classroom.

School Policies and Response:
Oak Grove High School has a comprehensive Code of Conduct that outlines expectations for student behavior and disciplinary procedures. The Code of Conduct prohibits disruptive behavior, defiance of authority, and failure to follow classroom rules.

In response to this incident, the school administration has taken the following actions:
- Meeting with Michael Johnson and his parents to discuss the incident and consequences
- Implementing a one-week suspension for Michael Johnson
- Developing a behavior intervention plan to support Michael Johnson's successful return to the classroom
- Providing counseling services to address any underlying issues contributing to the disruptive behavior

The school is committed to maintaining a safe and positive learning environment for all students and staff. The administration will review the incident and consider any necessary updates to policies or procedures to prevent similar situations from occurring in the future.

Conclusion:
This incident report documents the events that took place on March 15, 2023, at Oak Grove High School.
