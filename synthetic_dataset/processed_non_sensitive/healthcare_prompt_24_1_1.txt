# Exclusions and Limitations

While Acme Health Solutions strives to provide comprehensive healthcare coverage, there are certain services and treatments that are excluded from our plans or have specific limitations. This section outlines the key exclusions and limitations of your coverage.

## Excluded Services

The following services are not covered under your Acme Health Solutions plan:

1. Cosmetic procedures, including but not limited to, elective or medically unnecessary surgery, treatments, or medications to improve appearance.
2. Experimental or investigational treatments, devices, or procedures that are not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA).
3. Routine foot care, such as the treatment of corns, calluses, or toenail trimming, unless medically necessary for the treatment of a underlying condition.
4. Infertility treatments, including in-vitro fertilization, artificial insemination, and related services.
5. Hearing aids and related services, except for children under the age of 18.
6. Dental care, except for emergency treatment to alleviate pain and suffering, and for children under the age of 19 (refer to your dental coverage for more details).
7. Vision care, except for routine eye exams and the provision of eyeglasses or contact lenses (refer to your vision coverage for more details).
8. Private-duty nursing services, unless medically necessary and pre-approved by Acme Health Solutions.
9. Custodial care, such as assistance with activities of daily living, that does not require the skills of qualified medical personnel.
10. Services or supplies that are not medically necessary, as determined by Acme Health Solutions in accordance with generally accepted medical standards.

## Limitations

In addition to the excluded services, your Acme Health Solutions plan may have the following limitations:

1. Preauthorization requirements: Certain services, such as inpatient hospitalizations, advanced imaging tests, and some outpatient procedures, may require prior approval from Acme Health Solutions before they are provided.
2. Benefit limits: There may be annual or lifetime limits on the number of visits, days of care, or dollar amounts for specific services, such as physical therapy, mental health, or substance abuse treatment.
3. Out-of-network coverage: When you receive care from providers outside of the Acme Health Solutions network, you may be subject to higher out-of-pocket costs, such as higher deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance rates.
4. Prescription drug coverage: Your plan may have a formulary, which is a list of covered prescription drugs. Medications not on the formulary may not be covered, or you may be required to pay a higher cost-sharing amount.
5. Pre-existing condition exclusions: Your plan may have a waiting period or exclusion for pre-existing conditions, depending on your state's regulations and the effective date of your coverage.

It's important to review your Acme Health Solutions member handbook or contact our Member Services team for a comprehensive understanding of the exclusions and limitations that apply to your specific plan. If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

# Out-of-Pocket Costs

As a member of Acme Health Solutions, it's important to understand your out-of-pocket costs for the healthcare services you receive. This section provides an overview of the various cost-sharing elements that may apply to your coverage.

## Deductible

Your deductible is the amount you must pay out-of-pocket for covered healthcare services before your Acme Health Solutions plan begins to pay its share. Your individual deductible is $500, and your family deductible is $1,000.

Once you've met your deductible, you'll only be responsible for paying a portion of the costs through copayments and coinsurance.

## Copayments

A copayment, or "copay," is a fixed dollar amount you pay for a specific healthcare service or prescription drug. Your copayment amounts are as follows:

- Primary care visit: $30
- Specialist visit: $40
- Urgent care visit: $50
- Emergency room visit: $100
- Generic prescription drug: $10
- Preferred brand prescription drug: $30
- Non-preferred brand prescription drug: $50

## Coinsurance

Coinsurance is the percentage of the cost of a covered healthcare service that you must pay after meeting your deductible. Your coinsurance rates are as follows:

- Inpatient care: 20%
- Outpatient care: 20%
- Emergency care: 20%
- Diagnostic tests and lab services: 20%
- Rehabilitation and therapy services: 20%
- Prescription drugs: 20%

## Out-of-Pocket Maximum

Your out-of-pocket maximum is the highest amount you'll have to pay for covered healthcare services in a plan year. Once you reach this limit, Acme Health Solutions will pay 100% of the remaining covered costs for the rest of the year.

Your individual out-of-pocket maximum is $4,000, and your family out-of-pocket maximum is $8,000.

## Understanding Your Costs

It's important to keep in mind that your out-of-pocket costs may vary depending on the type of service, the provider you choose, and whether the service is in-network or out-of-network.

To get the most accurate estimate of your costs, we recommend that you review your Acme Health Solutions member handbook, contact our Member Services team, or use our online cost estimator tool.

If you have any questions or need assistance understanding your out-of-pocket costs, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. We're here to help you navigate your healthcare coverage and ensure you get the most value from your Acme Health Solutions plan.

# Appeals Process

At Acme Health Solutions, we understand that there may be instances where you disagree with a decision we've made regarding your healthcare coverage or claims. In such cases, you have the right to file an appeal to request a review of our decision.

## Types of Appeals

There are two main types of appeals you can file with Acme Health Solutions:

1. **Utilization Review Appeals**: These appeals involve decisions made by Acme Health Solutions regarding the medical necessity, appropriateness, or experimental/investigational nature of a particular treatment or service.

2. **Adverse Benefit Determination Appeals**: These appeals involve decisions made by Acme Health Solutions to deny, reduce, or terminate coverage for a healthcare service or claim.

## Appeal Process

If you wish to file an appeal, you can do so by following these steps:

1. **Submit a Written Request**: You must submit a written request for an appeal, including a detailed explanation of why you disagree with our decision and any supporting documentation. You can submit your appeal request by mail, fax, or through our online member portal.

2. **Timeframe for Submission**: You generally have 180 days from the date of our initial decision to submit your appeal request.

3. **Internal Review**: Your appeal will be reviewed by a team of healthcare professionals and plan administrators who were not involved in the original decision. They will carefully consider all the information you provide and make a determination.

4. **Timeframe for Decision**: Acme Health Solutions will generally provide you with a decision on your appeal within 30 days for a standard appeal or 72 hours for an expedited appeal (in cases where waiting the standard timeframe could seriously jeopardize your health or life).

5. **External Review**: If you disagree with the outcome of your internal appeal, you may have the right to request an external review by an independent third-party organization. Acme Health Solutions will provide you with information on how to initiate this process.

## Assistance with Appeals

We understand that navigating the appeals process can be complex and overwhelming. That's why we encourage you to reach out to our Member Services team if you have any questions or need assistance with your appeal. Our knowledgeable representatives are here to guide you through the process and ensure your voice is heard.

Remember, your right to appeal is an important part of your healthcare coverage, and Acme Health Solutions is committed to ensuring a fair and transparent process. If you have any concerns or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
