Westwood School District Budget Proposal for 2023-2024

Introduction:
Westwood School District is committed to providing an exceptional educational experience for all students. This budget proposal outlines the financial plans and priorities for the 2023-2024 academic year, ensuring that our resources are allocated effectively to support student success, staff development, and infrastructure improvements.

Instructional Programs:
1. Curriculum Enhancement: $250,000
   - Invest in new textbooks and digital resources across core subjects to align with state standards.
   - Provide professional development opportunities for teachers to enhance their instructional strategies.

2. Technology Integration: $400,000
   - Upgrade classroom technology, including interactive whiteboards, laptops, and software subscriptions.
   - Invest in a robust online learning platform to support blended and remote learning initiatives.

Student Support Services:
1. Special Education: $600,000
   - Hire additional special education teachers and paraprofessionals to support students with diverse needs.
   - Provide specialized training for staff to implement evidence-based interventions.

2. Counseling and Mental Health: $350,000
   - Increase the number of school counselors and psychologists to address students' social-emotional needs.
   - Implement a comprehensive mental health awareness program for students, staff, and families.

Facilities and Operations:
1. School Safety and Security: $200,000
   - Install additional security cameras and access control systems across all school buildings.
   - Conduct safety audits and provide training for staff on emergency preparedness.

2. Building Maintenance and Repairs: $500,000
   - Address deferred maintenance issues, including HVAC system upgrades, roof repairs, and playground renovations.
   - Implement energy-efficient initiatives to reduce operational costs and promote sustainability.

Revenue Sources:
The district plans to allocate funds from the following sources:
- State Education Funding: $1,500,000
- Local Property Taxes: $600,000
- Federal Grants: $200,000

Additionally, the district will apply for competitive grants and seek partnerships with local businesses and organizations to supplement the budget.
