Benefit Summary

Preventive Care: Your plan covers preventive services such as annual physicals, routine screenings, immunizations, etc. at 100% with no cost to you when receiving care from an in-network provider.

Office Visits: You'll pay a $25 copay to see your primary care physician. Specialist visits have a $40 copay.

Emergency Room: ER visits are subject to a $250 copay, waived if you are admitted to the hospital.

Inpatient Hospital: You pay a $500 deductible per admission, then 20% coinsurance.

Outpatient Surgery: Outpatient procedures done at a hospital or ambulatory surgical center have a $300 facility copay.

Prescriptions: Tier 1 (generic) drugs have an $10 copay, Tier 2 (preferred) drugs are $35, and Tier 3 (non-preferred) drugs are $60 for a 30-day supply.

Out-of-Pocket Costs

In addition to the copays and coinsurance outlined above, your plan has the following out-of-pocket costs for the 2023 calendar year:

Deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
Coinsurance Maximum: $3,500 individual / $7,000 family 
Out-of-Pocket Maximum: $6,500 individual / $13,000 family

Your deductible applies to most services with the exception of preventive care and prescription drugs. Once you have paid the full deductible amount for the year, you will only be responsible for copays and coinsurance up to the out-of-pocket maximum. After the out-of-pocket max is met, your plan covers eligible expenses at 100%.

Be sure to only visit providers and facilities that are In-Network, as your out-of-pocket costs will be significantly higher if you see an Out-of-Network doctor or go to an Out-of-Network hospital or clinic.

Keep in mind, your out-of-pocket responsibility "resets" every January 1st based on the new year's deductible and out-of-pocket limits.

Appeals Process

If your claim is denied a second time during the internal appeal process, you can then request an external review. This means an independent third-party organization will review your claim. Their decision is binding for both you and Acme Healthcare.
