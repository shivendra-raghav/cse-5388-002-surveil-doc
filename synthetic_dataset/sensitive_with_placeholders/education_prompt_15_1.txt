Oakwood Elementary School Safety Plan

Introduction
The safety and well-being of students and staff is the top priority at Oakwood Elementary. This comprehensive safety plan outlines procedures for preventing, preparing for, responding to, and recovering from a wide range of emergencies and hazards. It incorporates best practices from district, state, and federal guidelines.

Prevention and Mitigation
Oakwood implements numerous preventive measures to deter threats and mitigate hazards:
- Secure entrance with visitor check-in protocols
- Security cameras and alarms monitored by <PASSWORD>
- Regular facility inspections and maintenance 
- Student anti-bullying and anti-violence programs
- Staff training on identifying potential threats

Preparedness
Oakwood maintains a state of readiness through emergency planning and training:
- Designated response teams with clear roles and responsibilities
- Evacuation routes and off-site relocation sites identified  
- Emergency supplies stockpiled (food, water, first aid, etc.)
- Parent notification systems and reunification procedures
- Drills practiced regularly (fire, lockdown, severe weather, etc.)

Response
In the event of an emergency, Oakwood will execute the appropriate response protocols:
- Activate Incident Command System and establish control
- Secure campus and account for all students/staff  
- Notify district authorities and emergency responders
- Disseminate notifications and updates to families  
- Treat injured individuals and provide mental health support

Recovery  
After resolving the emergency situation, Oakwood will transition to recovery operations:  
- Damage assessment and facility restoration 
- Counseling services for students and staff
- Continuity of educational services and missed work  
- After-action review to update plan based on lessons learned

This plan is reviewed annually by the School Safety Committee. Community partners and local first responders provide input and collaborate on trainings and drills to test plan effectiveness.
