Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Healthcare! We're delighted to have you as a new member. This onboarding guide will walk you through the steps to get started with your healthcare coverage.

1. Create Your Online Account
   Visit our website at www.acmehealthcare.com and click on the "New Member Registration" button. Enter your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, and address.

   You will be prompted to create a secure password for your online account. Please choose a strong password that includes a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Your password should not be easily guessable or related to personal information.

   Password Example: <PASSWORD>

2. Review Your Plan Details
   Once your account is created, log in using your email address and the password you set up in step 1. Navigate to the "My Plan" section to review the details of your healthcare coverage, including deductibles, copays, and coverage limits.

3. Add Dependents (if applicable)
   If you have family members who will be covered under your plan, you can add them as dependents by clicking on the "Add Dependent" button in the "My Family" section. You will need to provide their personal information, including full names and dates of birth.

4. Set up Payment Method
   To ensure timely payment of premiums and avoid service interruptions, set up your preferred payment method in the "Billing" section. You can choose to pay via credit card, bank account, or another available option.

Coverage Details

Acme Healthcare offers comprehensive coverage to meet your healthcare needs. Here are the key details of our plans:

1. Preventive Care
   Routine check-ups, vaccinations, and preventive screenings are covered at 100% with no out-of-pocket costs when you visit an in-network provider.

2. Doctor Visits
   Visits to your primary care physician (PCP) or a specialist are subject to a copay of $25 for PCP visits and $40 for specialist visits.

3. Hospitalization
   If you require hospitalization, your plan covers a percentage of the costs after you meet your annual deductible. The exact percentage and deductible amounts will depend on your specific plan.

4. Prescription Drugs
   Our plans include coverage for prescription medications. The copay amount varies based on the drug tier, ranging from $10 for generic drugs to $50 for non-preferred brand-name drugs.

5. Mental Health and Substance Abuse Treatment
   Mental health and substance abuse treatment services are covered under your plan, including inpatient and outpatient care.

Limitations

While our plans offer extensive coverage, there are some limitations to be aware of:

1. Out-of-Network Providers
   If you choose to receive care from a provider outside of our network, your out-of-pocket costs will be higher, and you may be responsible for a larger portion of the bill.

2. Preexisting Conditions
   Certain preexisting conditions may have coverage limitations or exclusions during the first year of your plan. Please review your policy documents or contact our member services team for more information.

3. Cosmetic Procedures
   Cosmetic procedures that are not medically necessary are generally not covered under our plans.

4. Experimental Treatments
   Experimental or investigational treatments and procedures are typically not covered unless approved by our medical review team.
